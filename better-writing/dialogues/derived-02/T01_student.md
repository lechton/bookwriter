I do not buy it. Take the simplest case, doubling a counter. In React I can write that two ways. I can do `const doubled = useMemo(() => count * 2, [count])`, which is the derived flavor, or I can do `useEffect` with a separate piece of state and write `count * 2` into it on every change, which is the watch flavor. So from where I am sitting, a derived and a watch are not two different things, they are two spellings of "recompute when the input changes." Show me where they differ.
