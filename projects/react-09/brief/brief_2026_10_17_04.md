# Brief 2026-10-17-04: Revision 4 as a Separate Pass, and Data Written in Full

## Why

Revision 4 began as an experiment: draw the summary lessons' code in the full lecture's light editor, with comment balloons that point at a dotted-underlined token. It worked on Lecture 10 and then on Lectures 3 and 11. The owner then decided where its rules live: "I prefer to be in revision 4, separate, because they consume too much tokens. I'm concerned about the AI spending which lowers the intelligence of the actual content." So every content rule stays in the revision 3 skills, and the balloons get a skill of their own, loaded only for a pass over a lesson revision 3 has already finished. The one content point the pass uncovered, data described in a comment instead of written as code, went back to revision 3.

Casebook Case 19. ## Group 2: The revision 4 pass (#2026_10_17_04_group_2)

New skill `summary-revision-4`: the pass (copy an accepted revision 3 lesson, let the build list what needs a balloon, add balloons, look at the pages); which blocks carry a balloon and what it says, deferring to `code-comment-audit` for tokens and voice; widths (80, 68, about 46 in a column), with long lines broken as a formatter would; what the build draws (light editor, light developer tools, dark overlay, equal-height tree levels); the checks; archiving a copy when revision 3 changes. Content problems found during the pass go back to revision 3.

`src/build-revised-4.mjs` gains `checkBalloons`, part of its `[CHECK FAILED]` gate: every balloon has a `~token~` and every token a balloon, tildes in pairs, a balloon in every titled block of three lines or more and in every principle card, no three commented lines in a row, no comment in DO THIS or DO NOT DO THIS, and widths on every line before a balloon. Lectures 3, 10, and 11 pass; the unaudited revision 3 copy of Lecture 3 fails with 14 findings, which is the to-do list the skill's pass starts from. These checks used to be run by hand; the script spends no model effort on them.
