# Brief 2026-10-24 01: The River Pre-Lecture (skills v9.2)

## Why this batch exists

Every first draft of River Lectures 01 to 06 came out convoluted, and the owner had to ask for simplification each time. The evidence is in `notes/06-river-v2-persistent-convolution-evidence-brief.md`. A forensic reading of the `v9.1` River skills found seven sources:

1. The principle map wrote stories in compressed, code-heavy sentences, and the lecture copied them. Lecture 06's rejected first paragraph came almost word for word from map 06's beginner-mistake field.
2. The most important fault, a story told off stage above stacked code blocks, was named nowhere in the River skills.
3. The skills were written in the dense style they warned against: about 1 to 2 code spans per sentence and 26 to 72 parentheses per 100 sentences, against 0.5 and 0 in Lecture 04.
4. The skills printed the script's limits, "at most 2 backticked spans" and "28 words", and authors wrote up to them.
5. Lecture 01, written before the River voice settled, was named as a reference beside Lecture 04.
6. The tired-reader standard, the clear-explanation-not-summary standard, and the worth of every sentence never reached the River skills.
7. Heavy per-section requirements and one-shot writing of an 800-line lecture pushed the writer into compression.

## Group 1 (#2026_10_24_01_group_1): the pipeline gets a pre-lecture

River lectures are now made in three steps. Opus writes the principle map. Argon writes the pre-lecture, `01-01-md-PRE-lectures/v2/{NN}.md`, staging every section in beats that are the reader's real sentences. Argon then writes the lecture from it, one principle at a time, and may change anything that reads better another way, rechecking what it changed. Files: `README.md`, `v2/pre-lecture/SKILL.md` (new), `v2/map-principles/SKILL.md`, `v2/lecture-structure/SKILL.md`, `v2/lecture-voice/SKILL.md`, `v2/verification/SKILL.md`.

## Group 2 (#2026_10_24_01_group_2): the map is a fact sheet

The map's writer is not the River writer, so the map gives facts only: stories as numbered events, code names on their own line, and no drafted lecture sentences, principle boxes, spoken preview, closing line, or senior answer. `## Senior Answer` becomes `## Interview Points`. File: `v2/map-principles/SKILL.md`.

## Group 3 (#2026_10_24_01_group_3): the pre-lecture owns the content benchmarks

Twelve benchmarks live in pre-lecture Section 4, each with an approved passage and a rejected draft: the root, real bugs, everyday openings, the on-stage walk, plain lead-ins, one step per block and both sides of a handshake, bridges said out loud, cause before effect, believable examples with matching previews, figures after the text, sentences with worth, and principle cards in human words. The pre-lecture ends with a Content Gates report that quotes its own beats, including a ledger of every sentence above a code block. Verification Stage A checks it before any lecture page is written. Files: `v2/pre-lecture/SKILL.md`, `v2/code-and-figures/SKILL.md`, `v2/verification/SKILL.md`.

## Group 4 (#2026_10_24_01_group_4): code names as the reader needs them, and the limits out

Narration uses plain words for the parts; a code name enters a sentence when the reader must learn that name, which keeps the workspace's "name things as the reader sees them" principle. The script's numeric limits leave the author-facing text and stay in `src/build-river.mjs`. Snippet length becomes one step instead of four conflicting ranges. Files: `v2/lecture-voice/SKILL.md`, `v2/code-and-figures/SKILL.md`, `v2/verification/SKILL.md`, `skill-surveillance/SKILL.md`.

## Group 5 (#2026_10_24_01_group_5): good examples only from approved text

Every PROPER example is a verbatim passage from Lectures 04, 05, or 02, or the owner's own rewrite, with its source named. Counter-examples are rejected drafts or invented failures. The invented PROPER examples of `v9.1`, such as the news-page umbrella in lecture-voice 1A, are gone. Lecture 01 and Lecture 06 stop being references: Lecture 06's Principle 3 still narrates code names above its blocks, and the "conformed" Lecture 06 and Lecture 03 passages quoted in the convolution brief are not in the committed lectures. Files: `v2/pre-lecture/SKILL.md`, `v2/lecture-voice/SKILL.md`, `v2/code-and-figures/SKILL.md`, `skill-surveillance/SKILL.md`, `README.md`.
