# Brief 2026-11-09 01: One real screen, one idea

## Why

The owner found the openings of Lectures 20 and 22 convoluted for very simple ideas. Lecture 22 opened on a Slack Members screen with ten members, a search box, a counter, two buttons per row, and an admin line, listed four changes from one click, and then built a different screen, a team page. Lecture 20 opened on an Amazon Contact Us screen with a 200-character counter, a 19-character question, three React pieces, and a whiteboard, and then built a bigger form. Lecture 32 opened on a toy: a counter that counts the seconds by itself, which no developer builds.

Two lines from the batch of brief 2026-11-08 04 helped cause the crowding. Example 9 of the Opening Essence said the opening lists what the user sees change "with real numbers", and it ended by moving from the cart to a different page, a party guest list. The map's example page rules never asked for the fewest parts, for the case developers really meet, or for the same screen as the opening.

The owner's standard: one screen everyone knows, one idea, the fewest parts and words, short real content such as `John`, lists on the screen instead of plain text, engaging titles such as "Get your next month for $0.99", and the example developers really meet at work, even in a niche question. A parachute course does not teach jumping from a chair.

## What changed

Group 1:
1. `skills/v10.6/lecture/SKILL.md`: new section "One real screen, one idea" after "How to write each sentence". It opens with the real-case rule and its test question, then five points, then PROPER examples in the owner's words (`John`, the email title, the chat that scrolls to the latest message, the stopwatch), and the Lecture 22, 20, and 32 openings as counter-examples.
2. `skills/v10.6/opening-essence/SKILL.md`, move 1, second form: the screen has the fewest parts, and the lecture keeps building on it.
3. Same file, Example 9: the last paragraph of the owner's cart opening becomes "In this lesson, we will build this cart."; step 2 drops "with real numbers" and keeps only the changes the idea needs; step 7 says the lesson stays on the same cart.
4. `skills/v10.6/map/SKILL.md`, Section 3: the example page is where developers really use the idea at work, has the fewest parts and none fewer, and is the same screen the lecture opens on; map 22 is a new counter-example.
5. `skills/v10.6/figures/SKILL.md`, Section 4: previews are filled with short real names, lists, and a list before and after for a change of order.
6. Same file: the Lecture 20 PROPER example is relabelled as a model for drawing previews only.

Group 2: this brief, its row in `01_TOC.md`, and two rows in `skills/v10.6/RULE-TAGS.md`.

## How far it reaches

Every example in every lecture, in the opening and in the principles, now follows one real screen. Openings from the smallest code keep their shape; their code uses short real content. The owner approved replacing the last paragraph of the Lecture 23 cart opening, so the good example does not teach two screens.

Lectures 20, 22, 23, and 32 and map 22 are not changed in this batch. The uncommitted edits in `01-02-md-LECTURES/v1/22.md` were left untouched.
