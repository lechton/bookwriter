# Brief 2026-11-15 002: the chapter opens with a paragraph of three sentences in a box

## Why
The owner wants each chapter to start with a paragraph of three sentences that captures the essence of the whole chapter, in a box, to catch the reader's attention. The owner's first sentences for Lessons 29 to 45 were written by hand in intro/first-sentences.md, but no skill linked to that file, so the rewrite never read it.
