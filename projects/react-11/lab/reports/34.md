## Opening and example page

- Case: lab/cases/34/p0-start, run with `node lab/check.mjs lab/cases/34/p0-start` and with `--prod`. The starting project: `total` set by an Effect, `sendOrder` in an Effect of `OrderPlaced`.
- Claims: the cart shows `Socks $5`, `Hat $20`, `Total: $25`, and `Place order`; the dollar sign in `${item.price}` is plain text in JSX; the first render shows `Total: $0`, and a second render shows `Total: $25`; after a click on Remove on `Hat`, one commit shows one row with `Total: $25`, then a second commit shows `Total: $5`, and the user may never see the first.
- Matched: the screen lines `Socks $5 Remove`, `Hat $20 Remove`, `Total: $25`, `Place order`; on load, two commits, `Total: $0` then `Total: $25`; after Remove, two commits, `Socks $5` with `Total: $25`, then `Total: $5`, in the same frame, and only `Total: $5` was painted; the same with `--prod`; the Console is quiet.
- Differed: the lecture said "the user can see `Total: $0` for a moment". On the first load, both commits came before the first paint, in development and in production, and the only painted text was `Total: $25`. The docs' quote stays true: React gives the browser a chance to paint, but the browser paints only when a frame is due.
- Fixed: "So the user can see `Total: $0` for a moment before it turns into `Total: $25`." became "React gives the browser that chance, but the browser paints only when its next frame is due. On a fast page load, both commits often come before the first paint, and the screen goes straight to `Total: $25`. When the paint falls between the two commits, the user sees `Total: $0` for a moment."

  - "and in what happens when the thank-you line appears on the screen a second time." became "and in what happens when the Effect that sends the order runs a second time."
  - The principle list: "sends it again on every mount of that line: twice in development, and again after Back in production." became "sends it again on every mount of that line and on every change of `items`: twice in development, and again after a Remove in production."
  - The principle table: "and again after Back in production" became "and again after a Remove in production".
  - "A click happens once. The thank-you line can mount more than once." became "A click happens once. The Effect can run many times."
  - The two-item list "After Back" and "After a Remove", with the docs' quote about Back, became one paragraph about the Remove, ending "Here is the page in development after the click on `Place order` and then the click on Remove:", and a new preview: `Socks $5`, `Total: $5`, the thank-you line, and the Console lines `Sending order: 2 items`, `Sending order: 2 items`, `Sending order: 1 items`, each at `api.js:2`.
