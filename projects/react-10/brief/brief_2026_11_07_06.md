## 1. Why This Batch Exists

In Lecture 20, the `Principle N` titles coined in the map (`01-01-map-principles/v1/20.md`) and carried into the draft and final lecture are short, physical, and immediate:
- `Principle 1: onChange Sends Every Key into State`
- `Principle 2: value Sends State Back into the Box`
- `Principle 3: value Without onChange Freezes the Box`
- `Principle 4: The Same Pair on a Menu and a Tick Box`
- `Principle 5: A Controlled Box Starts with an Empty String`

In earlier lectures, or whenever Argon Gemini composes principle titles without an explicit exemplar, the titles turn into long, abstract thesis sentences that pack the mechanism, condition, and exception into the heading, such as `Principle 1: Replace Ephemeral Local Variables with useState So Changes Persist and Trigger a Re-Render` or `Principle 2: Every Render Gets Its Own Fixed Value, and Every Handler Keeps the Value of the Render That Made It`.

Two gaps in `skills/v10.4/` allowed this:
1. `map/SKILL.md` Section 4 defined the `- **Rule**:` bullet (`the rule in one plain line, with its condition`) right under `### Principle N`, without saying how to write the `### Principle N: [Title]` heading itself. Writers therefore copied the full conditional rule into the heading.
2. `writing/SKILL.md` Section 8 said `Headings say the idea in a few plain words`, but its table of `PROPER` and `COUNTER-EXAMPLE` headings showed only `###` subheadings, never `## N. Principle N: [Title]` headings.
