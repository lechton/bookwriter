# Brief 2026-11-07 (11): Skills `v10.5`, taught with moves

## What went wrong

The opening of Lecture 21 is one long paragraph. It tells again, step by step, what a one-line click handler already shows. The owner found it hard to read, and found the `v10.4` skills cryptic.

The `v10.4` skills caused it:
- Writing Section 1 said: "Being short is never a reason to leave out a step."
- Writing Section 3 said: "A result without its steps is not an explanation."
- The check skill told the writer to rewrite every sentence the build listed as long or as holding code. Lecture 21 got 47 rewrites this way. Older skills, up to `v10.2`, treated those lists as hints.
- The skills had grown to about 122,000 characters, with rules that said the opposite of each other and labels a new reader cannot follow.

## What the owner decided

- Make a new skill set, `v10.5`, and rethink it from the start.
- Bring back the verdict voice: "Yes, it is the exact same thing!" and "It looks the same, but it is NOT the same thing!". `v10.3` had removed it on purpose (#2026_11_06_02_group_4). The owner wants it back.
- In a passage about state, setters, props, and handlers, give the answer first. A short "Why? Well," may follow. A question may open a big idea.
- Keep the build's long-sentence and code-in-prose checks, as hints.
