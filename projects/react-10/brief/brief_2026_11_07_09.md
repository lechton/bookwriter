## 1. Why This Batch Exists

The Lecture 21 opening spent a whole paragraph narrating a one-line click handler that the reader already understood from Lessons 10 and 12. The diagnosis traced it to the skills. The owner had liked good examples, such as the Lecture 05 walk through the steps behind `StoryCard`. Each time, the model kept the example and wrote a wider rule around it, and the owner never saw the rule or what it would do to later lectures. The same diagnosis found the two mirror problems: removing such a rule tempts the model to delete the good example with it, and a new rule can sit next to an old example that teaches the opposite.

The owner asked to record this as the curse of AI skill writing. A first version went into every project and was retracted, because the owner wants it in react-10 only.
