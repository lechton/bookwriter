## Example: how to delete the wrong email

Here, a tick we put beside one email ends up beside another, and we delete an email we never read. It happens when a new object arrives at the top of the `emails` array while the DOM elements on the page have no key. The inbox below has no key on its rows. Read the four steps in order:

::: comic title="How to delete the wrong email" caption="The same inbox in four steps, with no key on the rows: what we click, where the tick goes, what the form sends, and what is deleted."
::: panel step=1 title="We tick “Invoice for March”."
```mail size=mini
toolbar: Delete selected
email: Bob Smith | Invoice for March | | checked good pointer | we ticked this
email: Anna Lee | Team meeting moved
email: Mom | Photos from the trip
```
> [!SAY] I want to delete the invoice, and nothing else.
:::
::: panel step=2 title="A new email, “Lunch on Friday?”, arrives at the top." tone=bad
```mail size=mini
toolbar: Delete selected
email: Sara Kim | Lunch on Friday? | | checked unread bad | the tick stayed in row 1
email: Bob Smith | Invoice for March | | | no tick any more
email: Anna Lee | Team meeting moved
email: Mom | Photos from the trip
```
The emails moved down one row. The tick did not: it is still in the first row, now beside an email we never read.
:::
::: panel step=3 title="We click Delete selected." tone=bad
```mail size=mini
toolbar: Delete selected | clicked
sent: sent to the server: `delete=4`, the id of “Lunch on Friday?”
email: Sara Kim | Lunch on Friday? | | checked unread bad
email: Bob Smith | Invoice for March
email: Anna Lee | Team meeting moved
```
The form sends the id from the ticked checkbox: `4`, not `1`.
:::
::: panel step=4 title="After the delete." tone=bad
```mail size=mini
toolbar: Delete selected
email: Sara Kim | Lunch on Friday? | | deleted | deleted, never read
email: Bob Smith | Invoice for March | | bad | not deleted
email: Anna Lee | Team meeting moved
email: Mom | Photos from the trip
```
> [!SAY] I deleted an email I never read, and the invoice is still here!
:::
:::
