# Brief 2026-10-18-05: Figures Stand on Their Own

## Why

In the Lecture 11b demo, two sentences ended on a colon to point at the next figure ("These are two different lists:", "The four steps below show what this does to a real inbox with no key:"), and one of them sat between two figures, carrying an idea that belonged with the opening. The owner: "Do not have this type of writing ending with : (add rule)", "Allow images to exist without leading sentence explicitly pointing to them", "Move this idea on top, as it polutes the space between".

## Pruned first

The colon was a rule of the skills, not a habit of the author, so it is removed at its sources:

- `summary-edition` Section 07, step 4: the setup "ends on a colon before the code" is gone; the code follows under its file title.
- Section 14: the setup sentence and its check lost "ending on a colon"; the check "Does each sentence that introduces a figure or code block end with a colon" is removed.
- `summary-figures` Section 06: the colon check becomes a check that each figure carries its own title or caption; its PROPER EXAMPLE (a colon lead-in) and its COUNTER-EXAMPLE now point to the caption as the cure.
- `summary-figures/blocks.md`: the build still keeps a colon paragraph with its block, for the older lessons; new text does not use it.
