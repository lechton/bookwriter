## Why

The first What is going on box in Lecture 11 had the right steps and the wrong words. Its closing line, "The text follows the email. The tick stays with the row.", chained three shorthands the box never defined. "The email" meant the object in the array in one step and what the page shows in the next; "the row" meant a line of the picture and an HTML element. The owner: "There is absolute confusion of the terms, you assume the person understands. What is the email? what is the row?", "The text follows the email... means what?", and asked to keep the phrase as the demonstration of a nothingburger. The owner's remedy: name the two things the reader already knows, a variable in the code and the elements in the DOM, in full, and repeat them; and say that they "should coincide one by one, when we change the one the other should change... that's why we need the ID in the first place!"
