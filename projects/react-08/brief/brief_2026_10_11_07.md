# Brief 2026-10-11-07: Everyday Settings in Skill Examples

## Why

Brief 2026-10-11-06 found that newspaper examples in the skills seed a newsroom world in the lectures ("Transit Accord Reached", bylines, editions, kickers). The rule to leave a specialist setting was added, but the proper and neutral examples across the skills still lived in The National Times. A writer copies the world of the examples more than the words of the rules, so the examples now use plain, varied everyday settings: a corner shop, a recipe page, a weather page, a to-do list.

## Group 1: Proper and neutral examples leave the newsroom (#2026_10_11_07_group_1)

- `ui-panels` Sections 01, 02, and 04: the canvas and files panel examples show a corner shop (`ShopHeader`, `ProductList`, `ProductCard` with "Green Apples" and "Brown Bread"; `OrderForm`) instead of The National Times, kickers, headlines, and bylines. The two counter-examples keep their faults with the same shop.

- `code-comment-audit` Sections 01 to 07: the card examples use products (`ProductCard`, `ProductPrice`, `price`, "Green Apples", "Brown Bread", "View Item"); "newsworthy target token" becomes "the specific token it explains". The column note for `onSelect` still holds, because the new names have the same length.
- `lecture-voice` Sections 01, 10, 19, 29, and 31: the opening examples use a recipe title instead of Hellen Jones and "Transit Strike Paralyzes Downtown"; the `children` example shows `<h2>New Park Opens Today</h2>`; "reporter" becomes "user"; `ArticleList` becomes `ProductList`. Kept on purpose: counter-examples whose fault is the newsroom itself (`lecture-voice` Sections 01, 05, 16, 25), the verification counter-example quoting a real Lecture 14 run, and the quoted failed Lecture 1 runway in `intro-examples.md`.
