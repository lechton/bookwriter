# Brief 2026-10-15-01: The Rule of Names

## Why

The first Lecture 1 demo of react-08 named its user Elena, who became Elena Ruiz, and its principle boxes used Ann Lee. The owner noticed that AI writers reach for the same few names in every lesson, so a reader stops seeing a person and starts seeing a pattern. The owner asked for an approved set of classic American names instead, in the style of Oliver Brooks, James Smith, Mary Jones, and Samantha Robinson.

## Group 1: The Rule of Names (#2026_10_15_01_group_1)

- `lecture-voice` Section 26B (new): every person an example names takes a first name and a family name from two approved lists of classic American names, or a name of the same kind; one name stays with one person for the whole lesson, and a changed name keeps its first name; principle boxes use a second person from the same lists. Proper example: Lecture 1's Mary, who becomes Mary Brooks, with John Hall in the boxes. Counter-example: the first demo's Elena Ruiz and Ann Lee. The section covers people only; code names stay under Section 26.
- `produce_prompt.md`: one line points the Gemini session to Section 26B.

The rule is stated as a positive standard with lists, not as a ban on other names (Prune Before Banning).

## Applied

Lecture 1, its pre-lecture, and map 01 now use Mary and Mary Brooks for the user and John Hall in the boxes. Every quoted result was reproduced again with the new names in React 19.3.1, Vite 8.3.4, and Chrome.
