# Brief 2026-09-20-25: Foundational Primitives Baptism and Anti-Robotic Narration

## 1. Context & Autopsy of the Defect

In `react 04`, while recent rules successfully improved architectural rigor (callback tracing, top-down assembly, negative counterfactuals), an unwanted model behavior emerged:
1. **Cryptic Checklist Compliance**: Model outputs started reading like an architectural auditor checking off requirements ("establishes contract", "fulfills contract from step 1", "owns zero useState", "seals boundary") rather than a tutor teaching a developer.
2. **Missing Foundational Primitives Baptism**: When fundamental primitives (such as `props`, `component`, `{}` evaluation windows, `destructuring`) debuted in early chapters, they were dropped into the text cold as bare buzzwords without etymological grounding, punctuation decoding, or syntax explanation.
3. **Robotic Recitation**: Narration in assembly steps became overly rigid and repetitive, opening every paragraph with "Look at line X:" and ending every step with "To recap this step:".

In comparison, `react 03` introduced concepts with deep, warm educational pedagogy: decoding every symbol, explaining what `props` stands for (properties) and what JS data structure it is (an object), explaining why curly braces exist in JSX (evaluation window stepping out of HTML into live JS), and contrasting custom React components (`<SiteHeader />`) with native DOM elements (`<header>`).

## 2. Technical Decisions & Rules Added / Revised

Under tag `#2026_09_20_25_group_1`:

1. **`skills/lecture-voice/SKILL.md` (Vocabulary 26)**:
   - Refined Rule 26 to mandate the **Four Educational Dimensions of Foundational Primitives** when a primitive debuts in the curriculum:
     1. *Everyday Meaning & Etymology*: Plain English meaning and JavaScript data structure (e.g. `props` is short for properties, a single JS object argument).
     2. *Syntactic Mechanics & Punctuation*: Explicitly decode symbols (e.g. destructuring `({ prop })`, `{}` evaluation window).
     3. *The Two Halves of Passing*: In `propName={value}`, decode left (child's prop name) and right (parent's live variable).
     4. *Platform vs Framework Distinction*: Distinguish native DOM elements (lowercase `<header>`) from custom React components (Capitalized `<SiteHeader />`).
