## Context & Defect Analysis
In previous lecture iterations, Stage D was introduced under a long, multi-line heading (`### Lessons from the Experiment: Naive Expectation vs Reality`) placed inside the standard left marginalia column (width 260px). In addition, the nested component role diagram (` ```component-code `) was wrapped inside a simulated macOS explorer window containing an outer border, gray background fill, traffic light window dots, and a redundant window title bar.

This design produced several layout and visual defects:
1. **Marginalia Squeeze**: In the narrow 260px left marginalia column, the Stage D title was forced to wrap across 3 to 4 cramped lines, crowding out vertical space and awkwardly colliding with full-width breakout elements.
2. **Artificial Window Clutter**: The enclosing window card and traffic light dots introduced unnecessary cosmetic chrome that distracted from the core structural relationships of the nested components.
3. **Vertical Gap Distortion**: Because standard marginalia headings float to the left, vertical spacing below the title was compressed and erratic.

## The Solution: The Exceptional Full-Width Stage D Standard
1. **Clean Universal Title**: Stage D is titled universally as `### Lessons from the Experiment` without appending speculative subtitles (such as `: Naive Expectation vs Reality`).
2. **Exceptional Full-Width Horizontal Heading**: Unlike standard body headings that float in the left marginalia, `### Lessons from the Experiment` is styled as an exceptional full-width horizontal heading across the page (`float: none !important; clear: both !important; margin-left: -260px; width: calc(100% + 260px);`). Because it spans horizontally across the full page width, it renders at its normal full length on a single line, providing generous, clean vertical breathing room below it before the content.
3. **Dedicated Page Milestone**: Stage D wrapper (`.keep-together.lessons-section`) starts cleanly on a fresh page (`break-before: page; page-break-before: always; border-top: none;`), removing awkward top border rules and preventing content from straddling page boundaries.
4. **Bare Nested Component Canvas**: The outer window container, gray background panel, traffic light dots, and window title bar are removed. The nested components (`ArticleCard`, `ArticleHeader`, `ArticleAuthor`) render bare on the page substrate with clean dashed component boundaries, strictly preserving their original centered dimensions and hierarchy.
