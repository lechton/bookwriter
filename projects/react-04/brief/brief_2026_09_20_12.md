# Brief 2026-09-20-12: The Motivation Voice

## 1. Context & Autopsy of the Failure

Reviewing the freshly produced Lecture 06 (`md-lectures/06.md`, the first output of the rewritten `produce_lectures.py`), the user flagged that the question-answer pattern repeats mechanically ("it repeats why? like autistic"). The lecture carries four Why? shapes in one body: two `⚡ IN THE WILD` cards both opening "Why does..." (lines 42 and 226), a "Why? Because..." inside the second card's prose, and the role panel's `main.jsx` entry ending "Why? To establish React's concurrent scheduler and take exclusive ownership of DOM updates inside the container." (line 217).

The tic is not the model being weird; it is three of our own laws converging, each individually reasonable:
1. **ui-panels 21** mandated the role entry's fourth beat as "the Why? question answered directly inside the same entry", so every role entry ends with the same marker.
2. **pre-lecture Grounding 22** mandates every card open with a bold leading question but offered no variety guidance, so cards land the same question word.
3. **pre-lecture Language 27 / lecture-voice Sentences 14** model a mid-prose "Why?" in their canonical PROPER examples, licensing a fourth one.

The user explicitly rejected a ban or a budget ("not any ban, clever change to tone down"). The chosen fix therefore changes the canonical shapes the agent imitates, not the frequency of anything: the instructions teach by example, and today every canonical shape around a motivation is a question.

## 2. Technical Decisions & Fixes

Design principle: invert the default. The laws now present the plain motivation statement (a "To ..." clause, a plain Because-clause) as the primary voice, with the question marker as optional seasoning the author reaches for when the surprise is real. Nothing is forbidden, nothing is counted.

1. **The role entry's fourth beat (skills/ui-panels/SKILL.md, Two Panel Modes 21)**: the beat is renamed from "the Why? question answered directly" to "the motivation answered directly inside the same entry, voiced either plainly (To isolate the parent from typing re-renders) or with a question marker when the entry genuinely begs one." The PROPER example (Lecture 40's panel) is untouched; its Notes gain: "The Why? marker is one voice for the fourth beat, not a required one; a panel where every entry asks the same question reads as a chant."
2. **Grounding card openers (skills/pre-lecture/SKILL.md, Grounding 22)**: one checkbox appended: "Vary the question word across a lecture's cards (What, When, Where, Why), because a hook repeated card after card stops hooking; the reader's ear tires of the same opener before the card's insight arrives." This is craft reasoning in the same style as the caps-emphasis rationale, not a prohibition.
3. **The spoken Why? in prose (skills/pre-lecture/SKILL.md Language 27 and its twin skills/lecture-voice/SKILL.md Sentences 14)**: the canonical PROPER examples stay verbatim; their Notes each gain: "The spoken Why? earns its place by following a claim the reader genuinely doubts; where several claims cluster, let all but one answer themselves as plain Because-clauses." The lecture-voice mirror is edited alongside the pre-lecture law because the two skills carry the identical example, and leaving one untouched would split the same law into two behaviors.

## 3. Why This Is Enough

The producing agent never counts questions; it imitates shapes. Before the batch, every canonical shape around a motivation was a question, so a lecture inherits four. After the batch, the canonical shapes are a plain "To ..." clause, a varied card opener, and a Because-clause alternative, so the next lecture naturally lands one or two genuine questions. The shipped Lecture 40 standard stays valid untouched (its panel keeps its Why? entries and is still the PROPER example), and no generated text is retroactively non-compliant.

- The second defect from the Lecture 06 review, the missing end-to-end data flow narration (a consequence of the batch 05 phased-flow removal leaving the flow with no mandated home), is NOT addressed by this batch and remains open for its own decision.
- No verification gate was added: this group changes default voices, not checkable constraints, and a gate item would have turned the nudge into a rule, which the user rejected.
