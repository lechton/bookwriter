The Pandemic of Charlatanism

§ I

We live in an age where the art of lying and manipulation dominates every sphere of intellectual life—from politics and law to physics and medicine. The "most trustworthy experts" lie. The non-experts lie. Pop scientists peddle falsehoods, and those who debunk them peddle bigger ones. Journalists spread fake news; their critics counter with faker news. Lies are the currency of the modern world.

With a straight face, scientists, legal scholars, journalists, and politicians lie about the most fundamental, easily verifiable facts. They lie about vaccines. They lie about statistics. They lie about the legal foundations of the European Union. They even lie about the definition of democracy.

Listen to the foundational catechism of the modern West: "In the West, we have democracy! In the East, China has tyranny! Why? Because every four years we freely vote for our representatives—the sacred cradle of democracy we inherited from the Greeks."

Now read what the Greeks actually wrote:

«Δημοκρατικὸν μὲν εἶναι τὸ κληρωτὰς εἶναι τὰς ἀρχάς, τὸ δ᾽ αἱρετὰς ὀλιγαρχικόν.»

— "It is democratic for offices to be assigned by lot, and oligarchic for them to be filled by election." (Aristotle, Politics, Book IV, 1294b.7–9)

Pause. Read that again.

To vote is to participate in an oligarchic regime.

They lie. Everyone lies.

Modern education is not growing citizens. Modern education is grooming clients.

The Semantic Trick of the Superstate

§ II

Under German dominance, official EU legal directives quietly stripped away the term "citizens" and replaced it with "clients", and clients are kept in the dark. Most Europeans have no clue they live under a European superstate with a European government, a European prime minister, and a European minister of foreign affairs: an executive branch of more than 30,000 civil servants passing laws top-down onto every member state, exercising the force of a real government. How do you hide an executive empire in plain sight? You carefully replace the word "Government" with "Commission," and "Prime Minister" with "President." It is the oldest trick in the book in public law, though few study public law as a serious science anymore. Unlike every national government, this European executive has no opposition, not even a fake opposition that pretends to fight the government in name only. Every organ in the European Union operates by manufactured consensus, treating dissent as an existential threat to the "European Project." At no point does the EU President sit face-to-face in public against an opposition leader to answer for failures. The role of the EU leader is not merely unelected; it is structurally invisible and wholly unaccountable. Anywhere else on earth, supreme executive power operating without opposition and debate has a simple name: an authoritarian regime.

Yet Western media relentlessly frames China’s President Xi as the tyrant. Whether history ultimately judges Xi as a dictator or a wise statesman is for the future to decide, but his governance is transparent in its direction—read his announcements from five years ago, and they explain the exact reality you see around you today. Everyone knows who governs China. Who governs the EU? Do you know today who actually made the decision five years ago to lock Europe into a conflict with Russia, or who decided to impose progressive liberal policies top-down even when national populations explicitly vote for the exact opposite? Throughout documented history, even the most absolute rulers felt compelled to stage referendums and pretend to bow to the popular will: Napoleon staged referendums whose results he was dutifully "obliged" to accept. The modern illiterate class of experts ignores how Hitler annexed Austria: by referendum through the popular vote of the Austrians—faithfully performing the oldest circus in Europe, the so-called "festival of democracy." Only the European Union dares to preach the gospel of democracy while openly discarding the popular vote in national referendums. The masks are off.

How does an regime get away with such brazen semantic fraud in broad daylight? By intellectually disarming the public long before they ever leave the classroom. We are constantly told that the antidote to manipulation is "critical thinking." Yet ask yourself: what is critical thinking, and why does everyone praise it while almost no one teaches it?

Critical thinking is not a polite classroom slogan. It is the ability to independently audit the theories of the "experts" in your textbooks. It is independent examination—wielding first principles as swords and shields to sever deceptive dogma from genuine logic and science.

To think critically, you must think in first principles. And to think in first principles, you cannot blindly consume theories in logic, law, science, and mathematics. You must be trained in the foundations of those disciplines:

  • Classical Logic: Mastering the ruthless mechanics of valid inference, deduction, and fallacy detection.
  • Empirical Measurement: Understanding the strict principles of empirical observation and measurement—the only antidote to statistical manipulation and scientific fraud.
  • Principles of Law: Mastering jurisprudence as a rigorous normative science. Law maps and measures qualitative ideas, intentions, and human acts with a structural precision far more developed and robust than any modern social science like sociology or anthropology.
  • Foundations of Mathematics: Cultivating the quantitative rigor required to audit formal claims and dismantle mathematical sophistry.

The First Normative Principle of Logic

§ III

Principle No. 1: Read the F***ing Manual

Before you repeat a media slogan, a pulpit cliché, or a "fact-checker's" catechism, open the primary text—the laboratory manual, the legal statute, the ancient manuscript—and read the actual words with your own eyes.

Inquiry Q: How can mRNA vaccines be called gene therapy products? They don't even alter your DNA!

Audit A: Read the f***ing manual! Have you ever opened a standard laboratory manual on gene therapy or read the legal definitions in the regulatory statutes of the FDA and the European Medicines Agency with your own eyes? Where in those texts does it say a substance must permanently alter chromosomal DNA to be classified as a Gene Therapy Medicinal Product? When a pharmaceutical corporation's own regulatory filings classify introducing recombinant nucleic acid to regulate, repair, or express a genetic sequence as gene therapy—why are you arguing with the dictionary on their behalf?

Inquiry Q: Did the concept of the Tao in Chinese philosophy emerge as a revolt against the arbitrary whim of a personal God?

Audit A: Read the f***ing manual! Open the Dao De Jing of Laozi and the foundational texts of the Zhou transition instead of consuming the caricatures peddled on Western and Eastern social media. Why did early Chinese thought abandon the capricious tribal deity (Shangdi) who ruled by personal favor, wrath, and sacrifice? When Laozi writes that "Man follows Earth, Earth follows Heaven, Heaven follows the Tao, and the Tao follows only its own nature" (Chapter 25), and that "Heaven and Earth are not sentimental; they treat all things as straw dogs" (Chapter 5), what is he establishing? Is that mystic poetry—or is it the birth of an invariant, impersonal cosmic law that strips even the heavens of arbitrary tyranny? And is this not the exact eastern counterpart to the Platonic Δημιουργός (Demiourgos—Creator), the divine craftsman bound by eternal reason and law that Christianity later inherited in the West?

Inquiry Q: Didn't Christ teach that humanity will be divided into two simple classes: those who have faith in Jesus and are saved, and those without faith who perish?

Audit A: Read the f***ing manual! Open the Gospel of Matthew to Chapter 25—the exact passage where Christ explicitly describes the mechanics of the Final Judgment—and read the transcript of the trial. When the King separates humanity into two classes, the sheep and the goats, does He ask a single person what theology they professed? Or does He judge them strictly by what they did—while those crying "Lord, Lord!" (Matthew 7:21) are cast out? Faith in its proper form (ὀρθὴ δόξα, orthē doxa—orthodoxy) is the internal motor of human action; works are the external, verifiable benchmark of election. Ask yourself a basic question of jurisprudence: if Christ ignored objective benchmarks and judged men solely for flattering His name, would He be a Just Judge—or an arbitrary tyrant? And why, at the climax of that formal sentencing, does Christ never mention "faith," invoking instead the objective standard of law (νόμος, nomos)—"Depart from me, you workers of lawlessness (ἀνομία, anomia)!" (Matthew 7:23)?

To pierce through the fog of charlatanism on these subjects—and countless others—you must master the foundations of operational benchmarks in logic. Only when you learn how these objective benchmarks operate across the classics, theological texts, and landmark legal decisions can you forge the discernment required to make wise, sovereign decisions in everyday life.
