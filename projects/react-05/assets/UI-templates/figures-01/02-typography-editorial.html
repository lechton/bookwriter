Senate Approves Landmark Clean Transit Initiative in Overnight Session

ER
Elena Rostova Senior Political Correspondent • Sep 22, 2026 • 6 min read

Following fourteen hours of contentious debate, federal lawmakers cleared the multibillion-dollar urban transit modernization package, unlocking unprecedented investments in light rail, zero-emission bus fleets, and municipal grid resilience across sixty metropolitan regions.

“ This legislation represents the single largest federal commitment to public mobility since the interstate highway acts of the last century. — Marcus Vance, Chief Transportation Analyst
