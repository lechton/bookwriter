## Group 1: Terminal Digest Sections Pedagogical Revision Standard (#2026_09_21_26_group_1)

### Philosophy and Defect Origin
During the initial pedagogical revisions of production lectures, `skills/lecture-revision/SKILL.md` focused predominantly on explanatory prose in Sections 1 through 4 (Density Breakdown, Thought Unbundling, Conversational Bridging, and Conceptual Boundaries).

Meanwhile, Section 07 (*Structural Integrity*) merely instructed authors to "preserve the complete anatomical skeleton", listing Sections 5, 6, and 7 as checklist items. This created an Exclusionary Fallacy where revision agents assumed that because Sections 5, 6, and 7 were structured digests, they should be copied across verbatim from `01-02-md-LECTURES/` without pedagogical rewriting. As a result:
- `### Where you will meet this` retained dry, compressed colon-appositive noun fragments.
- `### Glossary` retained academic dictionary prose with circular abstractions.
- `### Summary` retained dense, multi-clause interview paragraphs and textbook bullet points.

### The Standardized Fix
Under `#2026_09_21_26_group_1`, we codify Section 08 (*Terminal Digest Revision*) in `skills/lecture-revision/SKILL.md`:
1. **Holistic Scope**: Pedagogical revisions must extend from the title line to the closing table. Never copy closing sections verbatim from the canonical source.
2. **`### Where you will meet this`**: Author complete, vivid, active scenarios that stage a concrete dilemma a junior developer faces on the job, explaining what feature is being built, how the mechanism solves it, and what runtime bug occurs if ignored.
3. **`### Glossary`**: Banish circular definitions. Ground every definition in tactile physical reality: (1) what it physically is in memory or on disk, (2) what action it performs during execution, and (3) what runtime defect it prevents.
4. **`### Summary`**: Rewrite the `> [!TIP]` interviewer box into calm, confident conversational language a candidate can naturally speak out loud in an interview without reciting a textbook. De-compress `❒ Daily Engineering Reality` takeaways into clear, unhurried sentences that synthesize big-picture mental models.
