## The Problem

A review of maps 04 and 14, the two strongest maps of the set, found five gaps in the v6.4 format.

- **No field for the wrong idea.** Model principles used `Defect` to hold a belief ("A reader who pictures state as belonging to the function expects..."). Nobody can see a belief. The habit behind it was never named, yet `pre-lecture`, `principled-theory`, and `lecture-voice` all ask the lesson to explain why the wrong idea felt right.
- **No record of first appearances.** No map decided which lesson first explains the ternary, destructuring, or a template literal. Every lecture author had to read all earlier lectures to find out.
- **Doubts hidden where authors never look.** The unverified `NaN` warning in map 10 and the wrong `function` claim on card 04 lived only in a handoff note. Map 04 packed its conflict history into a ticked gate box.
- **Proofs spread across several apps.** Map 14 proved four principles on three components.
- **Dense prose and a self-ticked gate.** Map 04's scope was 230 words in two sentences, and its Senior Answer averaged 28 words a sentence. Every gate box was ticked by the map's own author, and the template title carried an em-dash.

The owner chose to rebuild every map from scratch in a new format rather than retrofit 22 maps.

## The Example Maps

Both maps pass `node src/diagnose-map.mjs`. Their Completion Gate boxes are left unticked for the owner's review.

| | Map 04 `#jsx_expressions` | Map 14 `#use_state` |
|---|---|---|
| Principles | 8 (5 Decision, 3 Model) | 6 (2 Decision, 4 Model) |
| Archived map | 6 principles | 4 principles |
| Ledger rows | 77 | 51 |
| Rows added by the Archive Check | 10 | 4 |
| Terms rows | 20 | 18 |
| Scope, average words per sentence | 8.1 (archived: 115) | 9.8 |
| Senior Answer, average words per sentence | 11.4 (archived: 28) | 10.9 |
| Proof components | `ArticleCard` only | `StoryCard` only (archived: three components) |

- **Discovery was not fully blind.** The session agent had read both archived maps during the earlier review, before Discovery began. Each map says so in its `Archive check:` line. A fresh session should build the remaining maps.
- **New principles in map 04.** `runs-during-render` (a Model: braces are evaluated once per render, and reassigning a variable after the JSX line changes nothing) and `line-break-space` (formerly a Supporting row) became principles. The Archive Check added ten rows, among them `onClick={handleSave()}` (Q13), missing nested data (Q10), and three common.md Caveats the first pass had skipped.
- **New principles in map 14.** The two reasons react.dev gives for a local variable failing became two Model principles, `assignment-renders-nothing` and `fresh-each-render`, because code can pass one and fail the other: a module-level `let` survives renders but still triggers none. `state-lives-in-react` covers the second half of the interview question, how `useState` remembers.
- **Verified behaviors.** All proofs ran in React 19.3.0. The `NaN` warning that archived map 10 could not confirm is reproduced: "Received NaN for the `children` attribute. If this is expected, cast the value to a string." It appears only when `NaN` is the only child.
- **Card conflicts reported in the maps, cards unchanged.** Card 04 lists `function` among statements that braces reject, but a function expression compiles. Card 04's naive `{if (admin) ...}` uses a variable its experiment does not declare. Card 14's Friction uses `ArticleCard` and its experiment `StoryCard`.
- **Skill example aligned.** The Terms PROPER EXAMPLE in `map-principles` 07 now quotes map 04's real ternary, `readTime > 10 ? 'Long read' : 'Quick read'`.
- **Terms that later maps inherit.** Map 04 makes the ternary, the template literal, `null` and `undefined`, `NaN`, arrays, object literals, `Date`, and `StrictMode` `New` at Q04. Map 14 makes array destructuring, `useState`, Hook, setter, and initializer function `New` at Q14. Maps 01 to 03 do not exist yet, so the `Recall` rows that point at Q01 to Q03 rest on the cards and the archived maps; the series run should confirm them.
