# Architectural Decision Brief: 2026-10-03-05: Console Log Payload Targeting, 10 Golden Comment Exemplars, and Conversational Clause Standard

## Executive Summary
This batch updates `skills/v6.4/code-comment-audit/SKILL.md`, `skills/v6.4/code-blocks/SKILL.md`, `skills/v6.4/pre-lecture/SKILL.md`, and `skills/v6.4/RULE-TAGS.md` under tag `#2026_10_03_05_group_1`. It codifies three critical rules: (1) Console log target underlines must wrap only the logged argument or payload (`console.log(~'content'~)`), never the `console.log` keyword or entire line; (2) One-word or two-word telegram comments (`// Click`, `// Bound`, `// Input`) are strictly banned in favor of rich 4-to-8-word natural mini-clauses explaining purpose or consequence; and (3) Multi-attribute JSX elements must split attributes across lines when needed to guarantee 35+ characters of horizontal breathing room for comments within the 80-character ceiling. It also establishes a canonical 10 Golden Comment Exemplars suite in `code-comment-audit`.

## 1. Motivation and Rationale

### 1.1 Root Cause of Flawed Comments in Lecture 14
During the authoring of Lecture 14 with embedded comment invariants, comments collapsed into telegram shorthand (`// Click`, `// Bound`, `// Child`, `// Local`, `// Submit`, `// Reference`, `// Parent`). Two root causes drove this collapse:
1. **The 80-Character Squeeze**: Authors crammed entire JSX elements into single lines (e.g. `<input onChange=... placeholder="..."/>`), leaving only 10–15 characters before hitting the 80-character line boundary. Under this spatial panic, the model compressed comments into 1-to-2-word labels.
2. **Instruction Phrasing**: The phrase "compact labels" in `code-blocks` Section 09 gave models license to emit terse telegram fragments instead of meaningful pedagogical annotations.

### 1.2 The Console Log Content Law
Underlining boilerplate keywords (`~console.log(...)~` or `~console.log~`) degrades visual hierarchy. The educational subject of a logging line is the payload being recorded—the event target, value, or state transition. Wrapping solely the payload argument (`console.log(~'Saved:', story.id~);`) directs the student's eye straight to the runtime artifact without boilerplate visual noise.

### 1.3 The 10 Golden Comment Exemplars
To eliminate ambiguity, `code-comment-audit/SKILL.md` Section 08 now includes 10 concrete, production-grade exemplars across common React scenarios:
1. Anti-pattern trap with `**WRONG:**` header.
2. Safe fix with descriptive mechanism explanation.
3. Event reference binding on button without instant invocation.
4. Inline arrow callback passing runtime identifiers.
5. Event handler inspecting `e.target` vs `e.currentTarget`.
6. Event propagation cancellation via `e.stopPropagation()`.
7. Native form default prevention via `e.preventDefault()`.
8. Multiline data collections annotated on the final property.
9. Conditional JSX classNames or ternary attributes.
10. Explicit named import from local domain modules.

### 1.4 Attribute Splitting for Breathing Room
To satisfy both the 80-character line ceiling and the 4-to-8-word conversational clause requirement, multi-attribute JSX tags must place attributes on their own lines when annotated, ensuring at least 35 characters of horizontal space for the comment.
