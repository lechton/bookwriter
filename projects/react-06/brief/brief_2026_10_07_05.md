# Brief 2026-10-07 05: Universal Everyday UI Engineering Problems, Map Story Decoupling, and Principle Term Bolding

## Problem
Two persistent quality defects emerged in recent lecture generation:
1. **Contrived News Jargon and Artificial Scenarios**: Despite instructions warning against idiosyncratic news terms, authors generated bizarre hybrid scenarios such as "Clara builds a free pass promotion desk for readers at The National Times". Root-cause analysis revealed that `lecture-voice/SKILL.md` Section 03 explicitly mandated grounding all code in *The National Times* newsroom model and a rigid character whitelist, while question maps (such as `01-01-map/17.md`) embedded legacy story names like `FreePasses` into their friction fields. Authors treated the map's story names as an immutable contract, forcing an artificial synthesis between a newspaper, a character name, and "free passes".
2. **Missing Bolding of Key Terms in Principles**: In earlier curriculum versions, key technical terms were bolded on introduction (`**props**`, `**unidirectional data flow**`) to visually anchor the principle and align with the marginal gloss (`>>`) and `### Glossary`. Following the October 6 adoption of owner-approved Lecture 14 boxes in `examples.md`, the bolding rule was pruned from `principle-definition/SKILL.md`, leaving subsequent principle boxes without bold letters and causing architectural drift against the Glossary requirement.

## Decision
- `lecture-voice` Section 03 (#2026_10_07_05_group_1) prunes the mandatory *The National Times* newsroom model and character whitelist fiction. It replaces them with the Universal Everyday Engineering Problems standard, requiring code examples to model universal web UI widgets (item counters, ticket orders, shopping carts, like buttons, comment inputs) that any basic computer user recognizes, with plain vocabulary (`count`, `tickets`, `likes`, `message`, `items`, `active`). A contrasting PROPER / COUNTER-EXAMPLE pair carries the doctrine.
- `lecture-structure` Section 06 and `verification` Gate 06 & Gate 21 (#2026_10_07_05_group_1) align with the universal everyday UI standard and prune newsroom requirements from the opening scene beat.
- `principled-theory` Section 04 and `produce_prompt.md` (#2026_10_07_05_group_1) decouple the map's architectural contract from its narrative scaffolding. The contract governs principles, order, ledger dispositions, mechanisms, and runtime defects; illustrative story names, component names, or fictional setups from map fields are scratch scaffolding and must be replaced with universal everyday UI elements.
- `principle-definition` Section 02 and `produce_prompt.md` (#2026_10_07_05_group_2) restore the requirement to bold key technical terms upon their first formal introduction in principle box prose (`**batching**`, `**updater function**`, `**state variable**`), ensuring seamless alignment with marginal gloss notes (`>>`) and the reference Glossary.
