# Brief 2026-10-07 04: Repeated Interview Question on Top and Interview Section Layout Gap Elimination

## Problem
In Lecture 16's `### For the interview` section, visual inspection of the rendered layout revealed two defects:
1. An unnatural vertical blank space (gap) appeared immediately between `**1. The Hidden Signal**` and its body paragraph. In `lecture.css`, generic marginalia styling (`main h3 + *, main h4 + * { min-height: 7.2rem; }`) forced the first sibling of `<h3>` to have a massive minimum height of 7.2rem (~115px). Because `1. The Hidden Signal` is a single line (~1.2rem high), ~6rem of empty space was forced underneath it.
2. The section transitioned immediately into `**1. The Hidden Signal**` without restating the governing interview question that the chapter investigates, missing the opportunity to ground the four interview beats in the exact challenge prompt the candidate faces.

## Decision
- `lecture-structure` Section 13 (#2026_10_07_04_group_1) mandates that `### For the interview` opens by repeating the interview question on top, formatted as a continuous blockquote: `> **The Interview Question:** [Question text verbatim from line 2]`, followed by the four beats.
- `pre-lecture` Section 11 (#2026_10_07_04_group_1) updates the `[interview]` taxonomy tag to record the repeated question.
- `produce_prompt.md` mandates repeating the interview question on top of `### For the interview`.
- `build-lectures.mjs` applies the `.interview-section` class to `<div class="keep-together">` when wrapping `### For the interview`.
- `lecture.css` adds `.interview-section` styles with `min-height: auto !important` on `h3 + *` (eliminating the 7.2rem gap) and styles `blockquote` with accent borders, subtle slate backgrounds, and uppercase eyebrow typography matching the curriculum style across default, black, and enhanced themes.
- `diagnose-lecture.mjs` exempts `> **The Interview Question:**` lines from the 20-word prose ceiling check since interview questions are verbatim curriculum assets.
