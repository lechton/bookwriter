## What Went Wrong

1. **A format became a template.** Batch 09 said *"when in doubt, decompose"*, nine of its ten exemplars were bulleted, and every bullet needed a *"(The X)"* label. The draft obeyed: one-rule principles were split into invented pairs, and the labels repeated code names in heavier words.
2. **An exemplar carried a false claim.** The side-effects exemplar said the server receives duplicate calls; the double call comes from StrictMode, which runs only in development. The draft copied it.
3. **The drafting session read finished audit output.** Every working lecture carried `~token~` markers, and the pre-lecture skill's own code example used them, so the draft imitated the audit.
4. **The rewrite of lectures 04, 12, and 13 was unrequested.** It has been reverted to the originals.

## What Changed

1. **Eight steps guide the thinking, not the final format.** principle-definition now walks through: find the decision, decide the shape, name each thing as the reader sees it, show what breaks, check the technical words, write the title, read it as a beginner, compare with the bank. Each step carries good and bad examples from lectures 1 to 13. The bulleted format stays the main tool for real pairs; plain prose serves a single rule; neither is the default.
2. **Side notes and definitions share their words.** Step 5 asks that every important new term appear in the definition itself with the same words as its side note (Lecture 1's *"state drift"* does this; Lecture 4's *"rogue zero"* versus *"orphaned 0"* does not).
3. **The blueprint shows its reasoning.** Every principle in the pre-lecture carries a `[principle-steps]` element answering the eight steps, followed by the finished box. The lecture copies only the box. A reviewer can see where a principle went wrong before the lecture is written.
4. **The exemplar bank stays.** All finished boxes remain, including `currentTarget`, side effects, `value` and `defaultValue`, and state snapshots. The side-effects defect now states what is true in production, and the rogue zero box uses its side-note terms in the definition.
5. **The audit works on the revised copies.** Audited lectures live in `02-01-md-revised/v6/` (with their HTML and PDF in the revised output folders). `diagnose-lecture.mjs` gains `--revised` to check them. The working lectures in `01-02-md-LECTURES/v6/` carry plain comments, so every drafting session sees token-free code.

## Philosophy

Prune Before Banning: the fix removes the bias (*"when in doubt"*, mandatory labels, tilde-carrying examples) rather than adding bans. Teach by Living Example: each step ships with contrasting examples from the shipped lectures. General Standards Over Micro-Regulations: no sentence or word counts; the steps ask questions and the content decides the shape.
