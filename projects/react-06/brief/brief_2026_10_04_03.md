## Philosophy & Core Decisions

### 1. The Anatomy of AI Over-Regulation

When an AI assistant notices a flaw in generated text (such as breathless run-on sentences, awkward word choices, or missing backticks), it reflexively invents new bans:
- *The Reflexive Ban Fallacy*: The model drafts negative prohibitions ("never do this", "never write that").
- *The Exclusionary Fallacy*: The model interprets positive guidance ("do X") as forbidding everything else.
- *The Decorative Example Fallacy*: The model leaves "Teach by Living Example" in checklists without actually producing or consulting living exemplars.

The result is prompt bloat, contradictory negative rules, and robotic compliance contortions that destroy the natural flow and breathing room of teaching prose.

### 2. The Five Principles of Principles

To permanently solve this failure mode, this brief codifies the **Five Principles of Principles for Authoring Principles and Instructions**:

1. **Prune Before Banning (The Golden Rule of De-Prescription)**: The first remedy for unwanted model behavior or stiff prose is pruning existing prescriptions and conflicts that force the model into contortions (such as rigid single-paragraph limits or mandatory templates). Never add negative bans ("never do this") when removing an artificial restriction solves the problem. Keep the instruction system lean and grounded in clear standards.
2. **Teach by Living Example (Examples Carry the Doctrine)**: Rules and checkboxes are merely indices; the living PROPER and COUNTER-EXAMPLE pair carries the doctrine, the pedagogical rhythm, and the tactile texture. Instructions must never treat examples as decorative. Every new standard or rule must ship with concrete, contrasting exemplars.
3. **General Standards Over Micro-Regulations**: Insist on broad standards of clarity, rhythm, and physical reality rather than micromanaging word counts, sentence counts, or mechanical templates. Allow natural structural breathing room (such as bulleted role decomposition for paired concepts) so prose adapts organically to the subject matter.
4. **Visceral Role Anchors Over Abstract Labels**: When defining paired, competing, or complementary mechanisms (`currentTarget` vs `target`, `state` vs `props`, `value` vs `defaultValue`), anchor them in intuitive human or system roles in parentheses (*The Listener* vs *The Trigger*, *The Snapshot* vs *The Request*, *The Controlled Input* vs *The Uncontrolled Input*) and concrete markup tags (`<button>`, `<span>`, `<svg>`), so the mental model is immediately visual and physically grounded.
5. **Name the Exact Platform Defect**: Eliminate hand-waving developer filler jargon ("preventing broken lookups", "ensuring robustness", "avoiding unexpected behavior"). State the exact physical runtime consequence ("returns undefined", "clears client memory on reload", "causes duplicate renders in StrictMode", "locks input into read-only mode").
