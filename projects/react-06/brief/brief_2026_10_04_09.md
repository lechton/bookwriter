## Root Cause: The Examples Were Outvoted

The rules asked for role-anchored bullets, but almost every other signal the agent saw pointed to the compressed paragraph:

1. Every shipped lecture in every version had compressed definitions, including the benchmark the prompt said to match (`v6/04.md`), the benchmark the skill said to read (`v5/04.md`), and the continuity lectures 12 and 13.
2. Measurable rules still demanded compression: verification item 20 ("2 to 3 sentences, ~35–50 words"), a principled-theory note ("2 to 3 calm, memorable sentences (35–50 words)"), and exemplar notes praising exact word counts. Lecture 14 met them exactly.
3. The new decomposition rule carried its own escape hatch ("for single unified rules ..."), which describes a 3-sentence paragraph.
4. The skill pointed to the exemplar bank "at the bottom of this file" while it sat in the middle; the bottom held the v5 benchmark and the template. Rule cross-references (Rule 103, 104, 111, 123) pointed to lines that had moved, and the "3-Beat Operational Rhythm" was defined nowhere.
5. The build rendered every definition paragraph as `<p>`; bullets came out as literal hyphens or broken italics, so the target form had never rendered correctly.
6. The blueprint was drafted from the map's one-sentence `Rule` field without principle guidance, Phase 2 copied the blueprint, and forced reruns were told not to discard the old blueprint.
7. Failing drafts were printed as full `> [!PRINCIPLE]` callouts carrying Q14's exact wording, making them the closest template to imitate.
8. The prompt demanded "`[PASS]` on all 8 invariants" while draft mode prints `[DRAFT PASS]` for Invariants 1-3, which pushed sessions into `--audit` and tilde markers.

## The Change: Prune, Then Make the Living Examples Agree

- **One short skill for the box.** `principle-definition` holds every rule about the title, definition, and glosses, the Golden Exemplar Bank, the failing drafts written as prose, and a Self-Check. principled-theory keeps the explanation, cards, and substrate.
- **Shape Follows Content.** The author lists the things the reader must tell apart; two or more entries means one role-anchored bullet each. The single-sentence exception requires a list with one entry.
- **No word counts.** The sentence and word quotas are removed from verification and principled-theory.
- **Renderable bullets.** `build-lectures.mjs` turns `- ` lines inside a definition into `<ul class="principle-roles">`, styled in `lecture.css`.
- **The benchmark agrees with the doctrine.** The principle definitions of lectures 04, 12, and 13 (and their blueprints) are rewritten into the role-anchored form; the stray blank line that split 04's Principle 6 callout is removed.
- **The map is data.** New maps carry Roles, Placement, Mechanism, and Defect fragments instead of a paste-ready `Rule` sentence; older maps are read as data.
- **The orchestrator reads principle-definition first,** has the blueprint author final principle boxes, has Phase 2 carry them unchanged, archives old drafts before `--force`, and states the diagnose draft mode.

## Example

**DO THIS:** an orienting sentence, one bullet per role with an anchor and a concrete tag, then the exact defect.

```markdown

> - **`e.target` (The Trigger):** the innermost element the user physically clicked, such as a nested `<span>` icon.
```

**DO NOT DO THIS:** one sentence folding both references together, with the map's wording and a filler ending ("preventing broken lookups").
