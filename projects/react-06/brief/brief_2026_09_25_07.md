# Decision Brief — 2026-09-25-07: Content-Driven Principle and Assembly Step Count Derivation Standard and Anti-Minimization Law

## Context & Motivation
1. **The Minimization / Lazy Collapse Fallacy**:
   Following the de-prescription of rigid quotas (abolishing "always exactly 5 principles" via `#2026_09_25_03_group_1` and "always exactly 4 steps" via `#2026_09_25_05_group_1`), an unwanted model behavioral defect emerged: the *Minimization / Lazy Collapse Fallacy*.
   When given an adaptable range (e.g. 3 to 7 principles, 2 to 4 assembly steps), AI models reflexively collapsed down to the absolute mathematical minimum (3 principles, 2 steps), regardless of the topic's actual complexity or the curriculum map requirements.
   For example, in Lesson 01 (*What is React and the Declarative Model*), the model collapsed the 5 foundational mental shift invariants down to 3 principles, and collapsed the multi-widget synchronization experiment (parent `SiteHeader` + `ReaderGreeting` + `SubscriberBadge` + `App`) down to 2 steps (`EditorialFeed` + `ArticleCard`), dropping the second widget and missing the core lesson of state drift across sibling widgets.

2. **The User Directive & Root Cause (`iii`)**:
   The user explicitly called out this failure: *"wait, the canonical was not supposed to be 5, but what the lesson itself supports, is it so hard to understand ? nnn"*.
   Examining root causes under the Golden Rule of De-Prescription revealed that while the rigid quotas were removed, the instructions *failed to provide an operational derivation standard* for how an author must determine the count supported by the topic.

3. **The Content-Driven Derivation Solution**:
   We establish explicit, content-driven derivation laws linking counts directly to `01-01-map/{nn}.md`:
   - **Principle Count**: Derived directly from the topic's inherent architectural invariants and failure modes. Exactly one principle per distinct platform law (e.g. 5 for Q01, 8 for Q04, 3 or 4 for focused APIs). Neither padded with filler nor truncated by laziness.
   - **Assembly Step Count ($N \in \{2, 3, 4\}$)**: Derived directly from the component topology required to prove the assigned experiment in `01-01-map/{nn}.md` (4 steps for multi-widget synchronization; 3 steps for container + interactive child + consumer; 2 steps strictly reserved for foundational single-component anatomy like Q02).
