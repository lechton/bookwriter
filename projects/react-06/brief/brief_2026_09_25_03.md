# Decision Brief — 2026-09-25-03: Zero-Gap Knowledge Flow, Operational Principle Definitions, Theory-First Foundation, and Meaningful Case Study Commentary

## Context & Motivation
1. **Zero-Gap Knowledge Flow & Proper Order Audit**:
   A recurring defect in lectures and pre-lecture blueprints was ungrounded jargon appearing downstream (for example, the term *Reconciliation* appearing in the Q01 glossary without prior definition in the text). Under the zero-gap knowledge flow rule, every technical term defined in a glossary or highlighted in a callout (`> [!WILD]`, takeaways) must be formally introduced, defined, and grounded in earlier text. Every callout and takeaway must make a genuinely new conceptual contribution, strictly avoiding empty duplication.

2. **Meaningful Pedagogical Commentary in Case Studies**:
   Identified and pruned the root cause behind mechanical, robotic line citations in case studies. Previous instructions directed models to point to line numbers naturally, which prompted LLMs to write autistic line-by-line syntax mappings ("Notice line 6 calls onSelect"). This has been replaced by meaningful architectural commentary explaining the lessons from the code, developer intent, cause-and-effect paths, and runtime engine behavior.

3. **Theory-First Foundation Before Principles**:
   Re-established a dedicated theoretical introductory section (`### {Theoretical Concept / Mental Model}`, such as *What is a React Component?* or *The Three Trees Model*) immediately before the principles section (`### Principles of React: {Topic}`). This provides students with a solid conceptual foundation and mental model before they encounter formal operational rules.

4. **Operational Principle Definitions & Anti-Authoritarian Language**:
   Purged authoritarian language ("you must do this", "must do that") from principles and guidelines. Mandated operational definitions explaining physical engine mechanics, browser behavior, and the explicit architectural choice, using Marcus's sibling branch isolation as the canonical benchmark example.

5. **Adaptable Principle Counts & Flexible Explanatory Substrate**:
   Abolished artificial numerical quotas ("always exactly 5 principles", "always 2 bullets"). Principle counts and explanatory accompaniments (code blocks, reference tables, figures, bullet breakdowns) are strictly adaptable to the scope of the assigned topic.
