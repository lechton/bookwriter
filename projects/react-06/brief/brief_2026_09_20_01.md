# Brief 2026_09_20_01: The Blueprint Staging Law

Batch #2026_09_20_01, group_1, working title: Blueprint staging law.

## The trigger

Lecture 40 shipped with clean prose and zero constructive demonstration: no Let's Design section, no Step 1-4 build, no files panel, no assembly pipeline figure, no architecture audit. The investigation found the principles fully present in the skills and the format heuristics fully present in the pipeline, but the blueprint had been authored without the structure laws in the room (the README load order loaded only pre-lecture and lecture-voice for pre-lecture work), and the heuristics never tested for the staging, so both safety nets shared one blind spot and the lecture passed clean against the wrong checklist.

## What changed

The load order now loads lecture-structure and ui-panels when a pre-lecture is written. The pre-lecture skill gained Staging 34: the blueprint must plan the five stages (files panel, assembly pipeline figure, Step 1-4 build, Direct Lessons, Architecture Audit table) and the 4-phase flow as concrete pre-elements, never as a reference alone. Verification gained the staging item in Pre-Lecture Gates 07, and lecture-structure Phases 01 gained the blueprint mapping checkbox.

## The philosophy

Structure is planned at blueprint time or improvised never. A blueprint written without the structure laws produces clean prose with no planned guided build, and the production lecture inherits the omission. Two safety nets with the same blind spot are one safety net.
