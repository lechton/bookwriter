# Brief 2026-09-20-26: Natural Prop Vocabulary over Contract Jargon

## 1. Context & Autopsy of the Defect

Following the recent rule additions for top-down code assembly and step narration, an unwanted model behavior emerged across generated lectures and revisions:
1. **Robotic "Contract" Jargon**: Generated text repeated corporate legalistic formulas ("establishes the contract", "fulfills the contract", "fulfilling the first contract", "establishes explicit contracts") across almost every assembly step.
2. **Root Cause Analysis (The Golden Rule of De-Prescription)**: Rather than adding a negative prohibition ("ban the word contract"), an audit of the skills revealed that the instructions themselves force-fed this vocabulary over 15 times:
   - `skills/lecture-structure/SKILL.md` (line 177) instructed: *"Step 1 constructs the parent container and establishes every child's contract in its JSX before any child exists, the middle steps fulfill those contracts one component at a time..."*
   - `skills/verification/SKILL.md` (line 195) mandated: *"...establishing every child's contract in its JSX..."*
   - `skills/pre-lecture/SKILL.md` (lines 183, 323, 330, 336, 346, 347, 353, 420) repeatedly prescribed "child contracts", "establishing contracts", and "fulfilling contracts".
   - `skills/lecture-voice/SKILL.md` (line 387) praised a proper example that used "we establish its contract".

This caused LLMs to treat "contract" and "fulfilling contract" as mandatory compliance formulas to stamp into every lecture step.

## 2. Technical Decisions & Rules Added / Revised

Under tag `#2026_09_20_26_group_1`:

1. **`skills/lecture-structure/SKILL.md` (Practical Example 19)**:
   - Replaced "establishes every child's contract" with "passes the props each child needs in its JSX before any child exists".
   - Replaced "the middle steps fulfill those contracts" with "the middle steps build those child components to receive and render those props".
   - Added explicit positive guidance: *"In early foundational lectures, explain child elements in Step 1 in plain, intuitive terms, preferring natural React vocabulary (passing props, receiving props) over corporate legalistic jargon (contracts, fulfilling contracts)."*
   - Updated proper examples and notes to eliminate "contract" wording in favor of "decide what data it needs" and "passes the callback function".

2. **`skills/lecture-voice/SKILL.md` (Vocabulary 26 & Guided Build 27)**:
