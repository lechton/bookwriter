## The Problem

An audit of lessons 01 to 13 against their maps (brief 2026-10-05-14 and its findings folder) found faults that start upstream, in the maps and cards the lessons are built from. Correcting a lesson against a wrong map would copy the error again, so the maps and cards were fixed first:

- **Map 04 was an older format.** It had no Bank Row line and no Sources Read list, its ledger was keyed to card lines rather than to candidates with owners and tags, and its Principle 6 taught the numeric `&&` guard that card 10 makes Q10's main subject (map 10's gate already reported the conflict).
- **Map 13 had no principle for propagation.** Card 13's Mechanism names `e.stopPropagation()` and `e.preventDefault()`, no other lesson owns them, and interviewers ask for the difference, yet map 13 left them out.
- **Card 02 and map 02 misquoted the lowercase component case.** They said the compiler emits `_jsx("articlecard")` and that the console stays silent. In React 19.3 the compiler keeps the case as written, `_jsx("articleCard")`, the browser lowercases the tag to `<articlecard>`, and React logs two development errors.
- **Card 05 and map 05 called `ArticleCard` a leaf** although it renders `EditorialByline`. react.dev defines a leaf as a component that renders no child components.
- **Map 10 said CSS-hidden nodes stay in the accessibility tree.** An element hidden with `display: none` stays in the DOM, where queries still find it, but the browser removes it from the accessibility tree.

## Proofs

Every behavior stated above was reproduced with React 19.3.0 and React DOM 19.3.0 through `createRoot` in jsdom under Node, with JSX compiled by esbuild 0.25 and parse failures checked with `@babel/parser` 7:

- `e.stopPropagation()` in a child's click handler stops the container's handler, `e.preventDefault()` alone does not, and the two calls are independent.
- `<articleCard />` compiles to `jsx("articleCard", {})`, renders `<articlecard>`, and logs "<articleCard /> is using incorrect casing. Use PascalCase for React components, or lowercase for HTML elements." and "The tag <articleCard> is unrecognized in this browser. If you meant to render a React component, start its name with an uppercase letter."
- The map 04 behaviors listed in its Sources Read (literal text outside braces, `src="{avatarUrl}"`, `disabled="false"`, statement parse errors, the printed-value line, "Objects are not valid as a React child", `style={theme}` equal to the inline object).

The leaf definition comes from react.dev `learn/understanding-your-ui-as-a-tree`, and the accessibility-tree behavior of `display: none` from the HTML and CSS specifications.
