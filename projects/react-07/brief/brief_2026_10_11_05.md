# Brief 2026-10-11-05: Plain Parts and Spelled-Out Mechanisms

## Why

Reviewing Lecture 7, the owner found two things the instructions allowed. The lesson's problem was an empty badge drawn as an "empty pill", which makes the reader picture a niche UI element before the defect can land. And the `children` mechanism was explained in one line in the engine's terms, "React puts that `<h2>` into the props object of `CardContainer`, under the key `children`", which is correct but cryptic to someone meeting the idea for the first time. The owner asked for the badge to go everywhere and for this sentence to be kept as an explicit bad example.
