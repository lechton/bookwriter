# Brief 2026-10-11-03: Rollback of Briefs 2026-10-11-01 and 2026-10-11-02

## Why

The two rounds of rules added on 2026-10-11 made the skills too complex. Gemini followed them literally, and Lecture 7 got a thin introduction, a broken flow (a question in the introduction, then a tool section, then an old interview paragraph, then the same question again), and an example pasted from the skills. The owner asked to remove what was added and reconsider.

## What was removed

Everything from brief 2026-10-11-01 except groups 3 and 6, and everything from brief 2026-10-11-02. That covers the "first sight" and "name and show" rules, the runway that teaches only tools, the foundation section, the one-problem opening, "the answer answers its own question", the Merged-row checks, the skill-example note, the reading gates "Questions left open" and "Name and show", the `[question-cycle]` line 9, and the diagnose-lecture warnings for runway terms, repeated code, and first sight. Every replaced sentence was restored to its text after brief 2026-10-10-01.

## What was kept

Two plain bug fixes:

- **#2026_10_11_01_group_3**: the recall list names only earlier lessons (`pre-lecture` Section 13C, `lecture-structure` Section 12, `verification` Section 21 gate 4); `diagnose-lecture.mjs` Invariant 14 fails a recall bullet that names this or a later lecture.
- **#2026_10_11_01_group_6**: after a statement's `;`, a comment is `//`, never `{/* */}` (`code-blocks` Section 09); `diagnose-lecture.mjs` Invariant 12 fails it.
