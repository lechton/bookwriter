Design description: A linear fan-out flowchart that teaches "define once, render many." On the left sits the single focal point, a mustard-yellow hero card with a heavy black outline and hard offset shadow, badged `{#snippet}` at its top corner, titled `figure(image)`, and showing a tiny three-line markup preview (`<figure>`, `<img src={image.src}>`, `<figcaption>`); a bold black track with a single triangular arrowhead fans out to the right into three neutral white render-site pills stacked vertically, each badged `{@render}` and each calling the same snippet with different data (`figure(hero)`, `figure(cart)`, `figure(grid)`), so one definition visibly feeds many call sites; a small black caption pill at the top reads "one definition · many renders" and a footer label under the hero reads "edit once → updates everywhere." The diagram uses the single-hero-card-plus-flow pattern, keeps exactly three counted elements (the hero card, the render-site group, the connector arrow), and carries its meaning through the asymmetry of one emphasized source versus many plain outputs rather than through color alone.
