# Path 04: The Mentor Senior Duel (Interview Edge Cases & Myth-Busting)

## Philosophy
Open directly inside the tech interview room. Bust the biggest myths senior engineers hold (e.g. "React was invented to be faster than the DOM"), while giving junior developers a crystal-clear, rock-solid understanding of what React actually solves.

## Pedagogical Invariants
1. **The Hook:** The classic senior interview question that eliminates 80% of candidates: *"If direct DOM scripting is 20x faster in microbenchmarks than React diffing virtual trees, why do production teams choose React?"*
2. **The Myth-Buster:** Dissect why "speed" is the wrong answer. Reveal the real reason: **human cognitive bandwidth and state-synchronization scaling**.
3. **The Microbenchmark Trap:** Show the benchmark where vanilla JS wins, and then show the enterprise application where vanilla JS collapses under state desynchronization.
4. **The Junior Bridge:** Explain how React's declarative model protects developers from their own memory leaks, race conditions, and DOM mutation bugs.
5. **The Senior Masterclass:** Articulate the exact vocabulary and mental model needed to ace a Staff / Principal Frontend interview.
