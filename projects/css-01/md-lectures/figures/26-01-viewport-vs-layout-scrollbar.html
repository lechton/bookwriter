THE VIEWPORT UNIT CONTRACT VS CONTAINING BLOCK LAYOUT
WHY 100vw EXCEEDS THE LAYOUT BOUNDARY BY THE SCROLLBAR WIDTH
width: 100vw (100% of Window Width) Overlaps Scrollbar →
+17px OVERFLOW
width: 100% (Containing Block Width) Fits Inside • Zero Overflow
17px Bar
W3C spec rule: Viewport units (vw) are calculated assuming scrollbars do not exist. In contrast, 100% evaluates against the layout viewport which excludes the 17px scrollbar.
