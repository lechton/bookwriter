MIN(): ACTS AS A CEILING (UPPER LIMIT)
MAX(): ACTS AS A FLOOR (LOWER LIMIT)
CEILING: width: min(100%, 60ch);
MAX CEILING: 60ch Takes smaller value
Opinion Column Grows with 100% width on mobile, but stops expanding at 60ch on desktop
min() Enforces an Upper Bound: Whichever argument is smaller wins; prevents line lengths from expanding past 60ch
FLOOR: width: max(320px, 50%);
Feature Sidebar Scales down with 50% width, but refuses to compress below 320px floor
MIN FLOOR: 320px Takes larger value
max() Enforces a Lower Bound: Whichever argument is larger wins; guarantees the component never shrinks below 320px
