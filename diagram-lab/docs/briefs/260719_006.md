## 3. Discoveries logged

- `base.css` line 7 imports Google Fonts via `@import url(...)` — the whole lab (not just `neo-brutalism.css`) depends on network fonts at build time, and the self-hosted Inter/Plex files in `assets/fonts/` are referenced nowhere. Decision needed: keep the Google dependency (Space Grotesk + JetBrains Mono) or switch to the self-hosted set.
- `.terminal` in `diagrams.css` is dark-background (print-hostile); the print-safe light version ships as `.console` in `metaphors.css`. Deprecate `.terminal` after migration.
- Badge-centered-on-title needs a small top margin on the first line (applied in exp-005 card 05); if it recurs, promote a `.bldg-badge` variant into the library per the gap rule.
