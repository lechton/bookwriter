# Decision Brief — 2026-09-26-02: Principle Ribbon Tab Compaction and Left-Extended Title Balance Harmonization

## Context & Motivation
1. **The Right-Heavy Lopsided Principle Layout**:
   Following the implementation of boxed principle callouts, visual inspection of compiled PDF pages (such as Lesson 05 Page 10) revealed an awkward visual imbalance:
   - *Oversized Ribbon Bar*: The blue ribbon tab (`.principle-ribbon-tab`) was hardcoded to `calc(260px + 20mm)` (~335px), which matched the exact negative margin of the header (`calc(-260px - 20mm)`). Consequently, the blue ribbon stretched all the way across the page bleed and the entire 260px marginalia gutter, ending only right at the start of the body text column ($x = 0$).
   - *Trapped Principle Title*: Because `.principle-title` followed the ribbon in the flex row, it was pushed entirely into the right-hand body text corridor (~380px–400px), forcing prominent 1.18rem bold headings to wrap into 2 or 3 cramped lines directly stacked over the definition card.
   - *Visual Disconnect*: The left side of the principle was dominated by a large expanse of plain blue ink with the text `PRINCIPLE N` pushed to its far right tip, while the right side was congested with titles, definitions, and code cards, breaking the horizontal balance of the page.

2. **The Solution: Compacting the Ribbon and Extending the Title Left**:
   - Calibrated `.principle-ribbon-tab` width to `calc(262px + 20mm)` (~337px total, extending 262px past the margin).
   - Inside the tab, adjusted padding to `padding-right: 18px`, anchoring `PRINCIPLE N` cleanly inside the marginalia area.
   - The ribbon ends cleanly before the definition card border, and with `.principle-title` having `margin: 0 0 0 16px;`, the title begins at the exact same horizontal coordinate as the card below ($x = 555\text{px}$ at 150 DPI, $\Delta x = 0\text{px}$), creating a seamless vertical alignment between the principle title and the definition box below it.
   - This achieves the exact balance requested: positioned more to the right than the 135px/195px/230px drafts, aligning vertically with the box below, while never trespassing into the right section.
