# Brief 2026-09-20 30: Role Panel Feature Scoping and Motivation Voice Pruning

## Group 1: Role panel feature scoping and motivation voice pruning (#2026_09_20_30_group_1)

### Philosophy and Defect Origin
Following the update to Stage D in Lecture 1, two defects were identified:
1. **Visual Clutter & Excessive Height**: The role panel contained 4 nested components (`App` ➔ `SiteHeader` ➔ `ReaderGreeting` & `SubscriberInfo`), introducing a 3rd indentation level and an unnecessary card for the trivial `<App>` page shell.
2. **Formulaic Question Chanting**: Three out of four entries followed the rigid formula `Notice [X]. Why? [Y]`.

Root-cause analysis revealed that:
- The canonical benchmark in `skills/ui-panels/SKILL.md` (lines 156-157) literally used `Notice... Why?` across two consecutive entries, prompting the model to treat `Notice... Why?...` as the mandatory syntax skeleton.
- In `lecture-voice` and `pre-lecture`, the rule explicitly commanded authors to ask `natural human questions ("Why?")`, concentrating model attention on that single token.
- In `lecture-structure`, the instruction to put "the hierarchy on record" was ambiguous, prompting authors to dump every file constructed in Stage C (including `App.jsx`) into the panel.

Per the Golden Rule of De-Prescription, rather than inventing new bans against chanting or prescribing negative prohibitions:
- **Pruned from Canonical Example**: Edited the benchmark in `skills/ui-panels/SKILL.md` Section 15 so that each entry models a distinct sentence construction: a direct purpose clause (`...passes zero props... to isolate the parent`), a conversational question (`Why? To filter`), and a temporal inquiry with participial payoff (`When does React read the data? ... keeping typing at 60 FPS`).
- **De-prescribed Question Token**: Softened `asking natural human questions ("Why?")` in `lecture-voice` and `pre-lecture` to `addressing natural developer questions (such as why an API behaves a certain way, when the engine updates, or what failure is prevented)`.
- **Feature Component Scope**: Explicitly scoped the role panel in `ui-panels`, `pre-lecture`, `lecture-structure`, and `verification` to the feature component hierarchy (orchestrating container and direct children, maximum 3 components), omitting top-level host shells (`App.jsx`).
