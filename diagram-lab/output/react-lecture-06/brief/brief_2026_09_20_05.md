# Brief 2026_09_20_05: The Phased Flow Removal

Batch #2026_09_20_05, group_1, working title: Phased flow removal.

## The trigger

Lecture 39's "The Two-Way Information Flow Lifecycle" section exposed the law as a force-fitted template: the title promised two-way information flow while the content narrated a mount, subscribe, dispatch, re-render lifecycle of a custom hook; Phases 1 and 2 duplicated the Fiber Node Memory Model section one heading later; Phases 3 and 4 restated the Direct Lessons the reader had just finished. Under the Lecture 40 standard the flow is now taught inside the guided build itself (DATA FLOW annotations, per-step recaps, scene-engine-punchline), so a separate mandated phased section had become the second telling, and second tellings are where the bad writing lives: the author fills a scaffold (Phase 1 through Phase 4, bold micro-leads, 3 to 4 bullets) instead of writing to the material.

## What changed

The mandated 4-phase information flow section and every law tied to it are removed. In lecture-structure, Information Flow sections 16 through 20 are deleted and Practical Example through Tables renumber 16 through 28; the Skeleton 11 body order loses the information flow slot. In pre-lecture, Staging 34's 4-phase checkbox and the Phased Flow Planning gate item are deleted. In verification, Structure Gates 16 (the 4-phase lifecycle gate) is deleted and the later gates renumber. The pipeline's 4-phase flow heuristic is removed from src/build-lectures.mjs, and its warn texts and comments now cite the renumbered sections. The section itself was deleted from the shipped lectures 39 and 40 and from their blueprints.

## The philosophy

A mandated scaffold earns its place only while it carries content the lecture cannot carry better elsewhere. The guided build now narrates flow in substance, so the phased section duplicated it in a weaker register. Runtime lifecycle narration remains fully teachable as ordinary prose: lecture 39's "The Fiber Node Memory Model" section is the proof, the same knowledge without the scaffold, and it reads better. What died is the packaging, not the permission.
