# Decision Brief — 2026-09-26-03: Plain-Concept-First Principle Definitions and Anti-Buzzword-Chaining Decompression Standard

## Context & Motivation
1. **The Single-Sentence Compression Trap and Buzzword Stacking in Principle Definitions**:
   During the review of Lesson 05 (UI as a Tree and Component Hierarchy) and earlier lessons, principle definitions exhibited an exhausting failure mode:
   - *Trap 1 (The Single-Sentence Compression Trap)*: Checklist items instructed models to articulate architectural choice, engine mechanics, and bug prevention simultaneously without setting sentence cadence. In response, models crammed 3 to 5 dense computer-science abstractions into a single breathless run-on sentence (e.g. `Distinguish the active runtime render tree formed by JSX composition from the static module dependency tree of files on disk. An imported component file only enters the active UI hierarchy when it is mounted inside a JSX return statement.`).
   - *Trap 2 (Cold Jargon-First Openings)*: Definitions led immediately with abstract technical vocabulary (`runtime render tree`, `pure projection`, `directed hierarchy`) before grounding the concept in plain physical reality, alienating tired readers.
   - *Trap 3 (Technical Buzzword Chaining)*: Definitions chained three or more abstract technical nouns in immediate succession without conversational buffers or plain developer actions.

2. **Root Cause Analysis & Golden Rule of De-Prescription**:
   - In `skills/v6.2/principled-theory/SKILL.md`, Section 04 demanded that the principle definition articulate the platform rule, engine consequence, and bug prevented in one statement without pacing rules.
   - The canonical PROPER EXAMPLES in Section 04 literally modeled this dense textbook style (`We conceptualize the component render tree as a logical hierarchy of React elements, completely decoupled from the physical HTML tags rendered in browser memory.`).
   - Applying the Golden Rule of De-Prescription: we pruned the single-sentence compression requirement and replaced the dense exemplars with the user-approved plain-concept-first pattern shipped across all 22 principles in `v4`.

3. **The Proper Solution: Plain-Concept-First and 2-to-3 Sentence Decompression**:
   - **Plain-Concept-First Pattern**: Lead with the plain-English physical reality or everyday developer concept first, immediately followed by the formal interview term in bold parentheses or attached context (e.g. `We need to distinguish the tree of **React components** (active runtime render tree) from the static **module dependency tree** (modules of the files we import).`).
   - **Decompressed 2-to-3 Sentence Cadence (3-Beat Rhythm)**: Definitions must use 2 to 3 calm, active sentences averaging 12 to 18 words: (1) what physical entity or choice we manage, (2) what the engine does or what does not happen automatically, and (3) what concrete action we take to eliminate bugs.
   - **Anti-Buzzword-Chaining Law**: Strictly forbid stacking three or more technical abstractions in a row; buffer technical terms with plain verbs and conversational anchors.
