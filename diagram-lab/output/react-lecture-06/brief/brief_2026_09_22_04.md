# Decision Brief: Banning Idiosyncratic News Jargon and Mandating B2 News Vocabulary Standard (#2026_09_22_04_group_1)

**Date**: 2026-09-22
**Rule Tag**: `#2026_09_22_04_group_1`
**Components Affected**: `skills/lecture-voice/SKILL.md` (Section 25), `01-02-md-LECTURES/04.md`, `01-02-md-LECTURES/05.md`, `01-02-md-LECTURES/02.md`, `01-02-md-LECTURES/03.md`, `01-02-md-LECTURES/06.md`, `01-02-md-LECTURES/07.md`, `01-02-md-LECTURES/08.md`, `figures/04-01-code-assembly-pipeline.html`, `figures/05-01-code-assembly-pipeline.html`, `skills/RULE-TAGS.md`

---

## 1. Problem Statement & Root Cause

Previously, idiosyncratic 19th/20th-century newspaper publishing jargon (`byline`, `wire`, `wire copy`, `lede`, `ticker`, `copy` for text) was used across lectures and component designs (e.g. `AuthorByline.jsx`, `NewsWire.jsx`, `wire-feed-flex`).
To a modern web developer, these terms are confusing, non-intuitive, and distract from the React concepts being taught.

---

## 2. Changes Applied

### A. Skill Invariant (`skills/lecture-voice/SKILL.md`)
- Strictly banned: `byline`, `wire`, `wire copy`, `lede`, `dateline`, `ticker`, `slug`, `kicker`, and `copy` (when used as text).
- Mandated common CEFR B2 news vocabulary: `article`, `news article`, `journalist`, `journalist profile`, `author card`, `article photo`, `headline`, `breaking alert`, `news alert`, `news feed`, and `publication date`.

### B. Lecture & Figure Refactoring
- **`04.md` & `figures/04-01-code-assembly-pipeline.html`**:
  - Renamed `NewsWire.jsx` ➔ `NewsAlert.jsx`.
  - Replaced `wire-feed-flex` ➔ `alert-feed-flex`, `wire-group` ➔ `alert-group`, `wire-headline` ➔ `alert-headline`, `wire-time` ➔ `alert-time`.
  - Replaced "wire copy" with "news alert text".
- **`05.md` & `figures/05-01-code-assembly-pipeline.html`**:
  - Renamed `AuthorByline.jsx` ➔ `JournalistProfile.jsx`.
  - Replaced "author byline" / "byline widget" with "journalist profile".
- **Lectures 02, 03, 06, 07, 08**:
  - `02.md`: "wire feeds" ➔ "news feeds".
  - `03.md`: `Byline` ➔ `JournalistProfile`, `WireAlert` ➔ `NewsAlert`, "editorial copy" ➔ "article text".
  - `06.md`: "live broadcast news ticker" ➔ "live breaking news bar".
  - `07.md`: "reporter byline" ➔ "reporter profile".
  - `08.md`: "byline" ➔ "journalist profile".
