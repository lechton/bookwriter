# Brief 2026-09-20 29: Component-Code Role Panel Single Source of Truth and De-Prescription

## Group 1: Component-code role panel single source of truth and de-prescription (#2026_09_20_29_group_1)

### Philosophy and Defect Origin
During the authoring of Lecture 1, the Stage D role panel (`Summary: The Logic of Nested Components`) was authored with terse, clipped, single-sentence entries that felt "thin, and in a loaded way" compared to the benchmark in Lecture 40.

Investigation revealed an instruction conflict:
1. `skills/pre-lecture/SKILL.md` Section 18 commanded a "5-second scan standard" and directed authors to "curate only load-bearing primitives" for `[component-code]`. These constraints were legacy rules designed exclusively for **Code Mode** (displaying syntax-highlighted code blocks).
2. When Stage D was evolved into **Role Mode** (displaying serif prose summary cards with zero code and the four-beat rhythm), the full definition and canonical example were placed in `skills/ui-panels/SKILL.md` Section 15. However, `skills/pre-lecture/SKILL.md` was never updated, leaving the pre-lecture blueprint author with conflicting instructions that encouraged ultra-compressed entries.

Per the Golden Rule of De-Prescription, rather than adding new prohibitions, this batch:
- De-prescribes the "5-second scan standard" from Stage D role panels.
- Establishes `skills/ui-panels/SKILL.md` Section 15 as the Single Source of Truth for the four-beat serif prose rhythm.
- Explicitly confines the 5-second scan limit and load-bearing primitive filtering to code-mode panels in Section 14 of `ui-panels` and Section 18 of `pre-lecture`.

### Files Modified
- `skills/pre-lecture/SKILL.md`: Section 18 refactored to point to `ui-panels` Section 15 for Stage D role panels, removing the 5-second scan constraint; checklist item updated.
