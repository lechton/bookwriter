# Decision Brief: Archetype 09 (Assembly Step Cards) Structural Invariant and 4-Column Grid Ban (#2026_09_22_06_group_1)

**Date**: 2026-09-22
**Rule Tag**: `#2026_09_22_06_group_1`
**Components Affected**: `skills/figures/SKILL.md` (Sections 12 & 13), `skills/RULE-TAGS.md`, `01-02-md-LECTURES/figures/*-01-code-assembly-pipeline.html`

---

## 1. Problem Statement & Root Cause

In several lectures (01, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 08, 09, 10), Stage B assembly pipeline figures were rendered as an ad-hoc 4-column vertical grid (`.pipeline-grid` with `grid-template-columns: repeat(4, 1fr)`). This compressed steps into narrow, cramped vertical boxes with dark top header strips and bottom tags.
Only Lecture 02 correctly used the canonical Archetype 09 structure (`.assembly-steps-figure`): a vertical stack of 4 full-width horizontal step cards (`.step-card`) featuring `.card-edge`, `.step-badge`, `.card-divider`, and `.card-content`.

**Root Cause**:
- Ambiguity in Section 12 calling Archetype 09 a "Horizontal 4-card sequence", which caused models to lay out 4 cards horizontally side-by-side across columns.
- Section 13 lacked an explicit reference to clone `templates/09-progressive-assembly-step-cards.html` and lacked an explicit structural invariant and grid ban.

---

## 2. Changes Applied

### A. Skill Invariant (`skills/figures/SKILL.md`)
- Redefined Archetype 09 as a **vertical stack of 4 full-width horizontal step cards**.
- Mandated cloning directly from `templates/09-progressive-assembly-step-cards.html`.
- Defined mandatory structural anatomy: `.assembly-steps-figure` container, `.step-card` row layout with `.card-edge`, `.card-main`, `.step-badge`, `.card-divider`, and `.card-content` (`.card-title` and `.component-tag`).
- Strictly banned 4-column side-by-side grid layouts (`.pipeline-grid`, `grid-template-columns: repeat(4, 1fr)`).

### B. Figure Refactoring
Refactored all 9 non-canonical assembly pipeline figures to the canonical `.assembly-steps-figure` structure:
