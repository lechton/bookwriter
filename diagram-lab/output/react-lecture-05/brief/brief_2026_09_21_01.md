# Brief 2026-09-21 01: Complete Skills Ecosystem De-cryptification and Holistic Rewrite

## Group 1: Complete skills ecosystem de-cryptification and holistic rewrite (#2026_09_21_01_group_1)

### Philosophy and Defect Origin
An audit across all skills in `diagram-lab/output/react-lecture-05/skills/` revealed systemic issues that hindered comprehension and execution:
1. **Esoteric Aphorisms & Cryptic Phrasing**: Skills across the board relied on oracular or pseudo-mystical expressions ("The term now has a body", "An X-ray: true, and empty", "Physical body first, spirit second", "The old wiring pays per keystroke", "The one thing", "The experience standard", "The default and the law"). While well-intentioned, these sounded like riddles to both human authors and LLM agents rather than actionable engineering standards.
2. **Fragmentation & Micro-Rule Redundancy**: Rules were scattered across dozens of overlapping sections (e.g. 31 sections in `lecture-voice`, 37 sections in `pre-lecture`, 34 sections in `figures`, 28 sections in `lecture-structure`), with sub-bullet clutter and multiple sections repeating the same fundamental requirements in slightly different words.
3. **Format Inconsistencies & Schema Drift**: Irregular section numbering (e.g. `15B`, `03B`, `04B`, `06B`, `26B`, `09B`), duplicate numbers (two Section 18s in `verification`), custom non-standard headers (`### 30.` and `#### ✕ FORBIDDEN...`), broken mid-word line wraps from prior merges, and inconsistent bullet formatting.
4. **Authoritarian Negative Language**: Overuse of aggressive prohibitions ("strictly ban", "law", "forbidden", "punishing") violating the Golden Rule of De-Prescription and the prompt-writing quality standards.

### Holistic Ecosystem Remediation
Per the Golden Rule of De-Prescription and the Prompt Writing Quality Standards (`quality-checklist.md`), every skill file across `react-lecture-05` was holistically rewritten into clear, rational thematic pillars with standardized formatting, direct professional English, and zero hard-wrapping:

1. **`lecture-voice` (7 Pillars, 29 Principles)**:
   * Stance & Perspective (developer at the keyboard, anti-engine voice, anti-brochure speak, empathetic anticipation).
   * Language & Readability (CEFR B2 plain English, 20-word sentence cap, explicit entity qualification, paragraph cohesion).
   * Explaining Concepts (3-part anchor: write $\rightarrow$ delete $\rightarrow$ browser consequence; behavior-first definitions; native default vs framework invariant).
   * Lecture Openings (line-2 interview question, 7 numbered opening beats from scene to promise, holding back the mechanism).
   * Callout System (`[!TIP]`, `[!NOTE]`, `[!KEY]`, `[!WARNING]`, `[!CAUTION]`, and `[!WILD]` friction-discharge cards).
   * Vocabulary & Baptisms (5-step organic progression for new terms, 4 dimensions for foundational primitives, standard React vocabulary).
   * Guided Build Narration (calm direct instruction, scene/engine/punchline comparisons, Upward Wire callback tracing, Negative Counterfactual architectural separation).

2. **`lecture-structure` (5 Pillars, 26 Principles)**:
   * Workflow & Phases (blueprint-first discipline, expanding verified plans, traceability, internal workflow gates).
   * Section Skeleton (canonical 10-part skeleton, document title on line 1, line-2 interview callout, semantic heading levels, Pre-Example Mechanism Bridge).
   * Practical Example 5-Stage Architecture (Stages A through E: UI Canvas, Assembly Pipeline Figure, 4 sequential collaborative steps assembled top-down, Stage D Lessons from the Experiment with Component Role panel, Stage E Architecture Audit Table).
   * Component Naming (everyday words, anti-pileup, role-based naming over HTML tags, standard genealogical hierarchy).
   * Closing Comparison Tables (three-column matrix, empty top-left header, code span formatting with `<br>`).

3. **`pre-lecture` (6 Pillars, 19 Principles)**:
   * Methodology & Philosophy (blueprint as first iteration, grounding from known to unknown, physical reality first, topic fidelity).
   * Technical Density & Grading (★ runtime mechanics vs ◇ conceptual introductions, expanded preknowledge recaps, one concern per bullet, pairing old solutions with real code).
   * Pre-Element Taxonomy (`[p]`, `[code]`, `[components]`, `[files]`, `[component-code]`, `[figure]`, `[grounding]`, `[glossary]`, ladder beats; substantive reasoning over meta-phrasing; 10-line code ceiling with continuation attributes; host element visibility).
   * Practical Example Staging (planning Stages A through E as concrete pre-elements, top-down assembly in Stage C, Upward Wire and Counterfactual circuits in step bullets).

4. **`code-blocks` (5 Pillars, 13 Principles)**:

5. **`ui-panels` (4 Pillars, 8 Principles)**:
