## The philosophy

In React's declarative model, data flows down through props, and user actions flow up through callbacks (inverse data flow). Establishing the contract in Step 1 is only half the job; fulfilling it in Step 2 requires explicitly completing the circuit. A child component does not own state and does not know what the state is used for; it only holds a telephone line. 

When technical prose says a child "simply invokes" a callback, it conceals the exact mechanical wire the student is trying to learn. Explaining the wire means answering where the function was born, who passed it, and what physically executes in parent memory when called.
