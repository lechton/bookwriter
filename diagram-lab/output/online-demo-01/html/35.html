Question. How does the $state rune handle deep reactivity within nested objects and arrays?

Answer. Unlike legacy Svelte which relied on explicit reassignments to trigger reactivity on objects and arrays, the $state rune is fundamentally deeply reactive by default. When you pass an object or array to $state(value), Svelte automatically wraps it (and all of its deeply nested properties) in a reactive Proxy.

This means that you can mutate a deeply nested property directly, or push to an array, and the fine-grained signal system will perfectly detect the granular change and update only the targeted DOM nodes that depend on that specific leaf node.
