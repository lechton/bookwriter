    Neo-Brutalist Technical Diagram Style Prompt
  
      A high-contrast, flat design technical diagram set against a clean, off-white background with a slight paper texture. All elements (cards, containers, lines, arrows) feature a thick, heavy black outline to create a stark, bold interface.
  
      The Color Palette:

        Primary Accent: A flat, muted mustard-yellow used for highlighting key nodes, special states, or terminal signals.

        Secondary Accent: A flat, desaturated cyan or teal used for intermediate connection points, status pills, or transformation indicators.

        Neutral Elements: Pure white fills for standard data containers.

        Typography & Lines: Crisp, solid black monospaced font for all text and code. Bold, thick black lines for connections, distinct from the outlines of the containers.

      Design Elements:

        Containers: Rigid rectangles with bold outlines and a heavy, stark black drop shadow (offset down and right) for depth.

        Indicators: Pill-shaped or circular badges with distinct fill colors and bold outlines.

        Structure: Minimalist and industrial. The main content is enclosed in a stylized browser window outline with three simple "traffic light" dots in the top-left corner. Titles should be bold, large, and underscored with a heavy black line.
