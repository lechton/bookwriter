Design description: A stark, Neo-Brutalist structural layout comparing legacy reactivity vs the `$state` execution model. On the left (Legacy Structure), a large container labeled "Component Tree" holds visually nested geometric boxes representing child components. A heavy line cascades down through all the nested boxes to hit a DOM node at the bottom, illustrating full re-evaluation. On the right (`$state` Structure), a standalone, vibrant "Signal Yellow" block labeled `$state(0)` sits outside the tree. A single, direct, horizontal heavy black arrow shoots from `$state(0)` straight into an isolated `<h1>` DOM node container, labeled "DIRECT UPDATE". The design strictly follows flat, geometric, architectural data propagation paths.
