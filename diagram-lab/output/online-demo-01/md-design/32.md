Design description: The graphic is a flowchart using a Neo-Brutalist aesthetic set against a light yellow grid background. At the top, a wide, bright white banner with a thick black border reads `$: { ... } (Reactive Block)`. From this banner, thick black lines branch down to three uniquely colored cards: a bright pink card reading "if / else Branching", a cyan card reading "Multi-Line Logic", and a bright orange card reading "Function Calls". Each card has a heavy, solid black offset shadow and bold typography, visualizing the diverse capabilities housed within a single reactive block.
