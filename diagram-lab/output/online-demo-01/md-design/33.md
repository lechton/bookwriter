Design description: The graphic uses a minimalist, asymmetrical Neo-Brutalist metaphor called "The Broken Cage." On the left, a thick dashed box labeled ".svelte BOUNDARY" acts as a rigid container holding a padlock icon and the legacy syntax `let x = 0;`, representing "Trapped Reactivity." On the right, the open white space represents "Universal Primitives." Here, a bright cyan brutalist node labeled `$state(0)` radiates explicitly with a "PORTABLE" connection out to a yellow `.svelte.js` file, while a heavy black arrow wires back through the dashed boundary into the component. The design avoids red/green validation colors entirely, using stark black, white, and a single cyan accent to draw focus to the liberation of state.
