Design description: A side-by-side comparison of two cards on an off-white paper background, teaching the single mechanical difference between `$state` and `$state.raw`: whether a deep proxy is allocated. The left card is labeled `$state(hugeArray)` and its interior is scattered with small proxy boxes, each marked "P", to make the per-property proxying visible at a glance; a small caption beneath it reads "proxy on every property". The right card is labeled `$state.raw(hugeArray)` and holds a single flat block representing the whole array with no interior structure, marked with the label "one value, no proxies"; a small caption beneath it reads "tracked only at the binding". Both cards share identical thick black outlines and hard offset shadows. The right card alone carries the mustard-yellow accent, marking it as the prescriptive choice for read-mostly data you replace wholesale; the left card stays neutral white because deep proxying is the default and the correct general answer, not the highlighted target. A single horizontal micro-label spans the gap between the two cards reading "deep proxy on" versus "deep proxy off". Exactly four visual elements total: left card, right card, the two labels under each. No arrows, no flow, no contrast row, no curved replace-arrow, no faded inset — the diagram argues its point by what is inside each box, nothing else.
