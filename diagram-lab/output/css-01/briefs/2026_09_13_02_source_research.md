## The authority hierarchy for css-01 (when sources disagree)

1. **CSSWG specifications** (`csswg-drafts`): normative law.
2. **Official documentation**: MDN first; official preprocessor docs (sass-site, less-docs, getbem) for their own languages.
3. **Books**: practical craft, subordinate to standards.
4. **Web Almanac `css.md`**: real-world data, never mechanism authority.
5. **Practitioner sites and repos** (moderncss, smolcss, defensivecss, CSS-Tricks): teaching phrasing only.
6. **Front End Interview Handbook**: answer format only, never authority.
