Design description: A vertical flowchart that teaches "bypass intermediate layers." At the top is a bold card representing the root component, badged `setVIPContext({ isVIP: true })`; below it are three greyed-out, stacked, slightly faded intermediate component boxes labeled "layout", "grid", and "card"; at the bottom is a second bold card badged `getVIPContext()` representing the deep descendant component; a thick, bright orange arrow bypasses the middle components entirely, arcing from the top setter directly to the bottom getter. The diagram uses a top-to-bottom layout with a curved bypass connector, keeps exactly three counted elements (the top card, the bottom card, the bypass arrow), and carries its meaning through the visual contrast between the solid bypass path and the faded, ignored intermediate layers.
