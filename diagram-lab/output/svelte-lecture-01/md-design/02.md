Design description: A three-step assembly line diagram illustrating the build step. Step 1 shows a raw `.svelte` file being broken down into a branching tree structure (AST) to map reactivity. Step 2 shows a CSS block being stamped with a unique hash (svelte-xyz) and extracted into a bundle. Step 3 shows declarative Svelte syntax being stripped away and replaced with direct, imperative JavaScript instructions (like document.createElement) sliding out on a conveyor belt.
