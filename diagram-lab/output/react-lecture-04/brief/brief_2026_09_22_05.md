## 1. Problem Statement & Root Cause

Previously, the instructions established *The National Times* newsroom as the running world but did not provide a standardized whitelist of character names. Consequently, authoring agents and models introduced arbitrary, ad-hoc names across different lectures and figures (e.g., `Elena Rostova`, `Marcus Vance`, `Sarah Lin`), creating narrative inconsistency and cognitive friction for the reader.

---

  - `Leo Smith`
  - `James Cooper`
  - `Ann Brooks`
