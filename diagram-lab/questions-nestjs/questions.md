# NestJS Interview Questions

A unified, dependency-ordered curriculum of **200 questions**: Core & Fundamentals (1–100) and Ecosystem & Microservices (101–200). One continuous numbering scale, sections stacked one under the other, so any question can be cited by its number (e.g. "write the lecture for Q47").
