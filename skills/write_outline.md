Principles

Legal cases should explicitly state their principle or principles. 

Legal cases should start with - ➔ **Principle**:, before - **Context**: bullet. If there are more than one principles correlated with the case should be all presented.

For example this is a problematic response (no principles mentioned)

````

- **[✔️ probable interference, ✖️ no liability] ★ Evans v Triplex Safety Glass Co Ltd (1936)**
    
    - **Context**: "Toughened safety glass" undergoes heat treatment to increase strength. Installation stress occurs when glass is fitted into frames - improper fitting can create pressure points leading to delayed failure
    - **Facts**:
        - Car windscreen shattered spontaneously one year after purchase
        - Injuries to Mr Evans, wife, and son
        - No apparent external cause
    - **Holding** (Porter J):
        - ✖️ Insufficient evidence manufacturer at fault
        - Suspected fault in windscreen installation process
        - One year ownership = reasonable opportunity for inspection
        - Installation strain could cause delayed failure

```

In this example, the missing Principle (Before the context) would be: 

- ➔ **Principle**: Manufacturer not liable where reasonable possibility exists that defect arose from intermediate handling/installation rather than manufacturing process
