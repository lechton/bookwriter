SUMMARY
In Svelte, the $state rune creates a fine-grained, proxy-based reactive variable. When the state is directly modified, Svelte's reactivity system instantly detects the change and updates only the specific parts of the DOM that depend on that state, ensuring optimal performance without a virtual DOM. This makes UI updates seamless and explicit.
