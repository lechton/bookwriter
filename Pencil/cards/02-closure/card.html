SUMMARY
A closure is a function bundled together with references to its surrounding state — its lexical scope. When createClapButton() returns, its local claps would normally be discarded, but because clap() and total() still reference it, the scope is kept alive. The variable becomes private state, reachable only through the returned functions — encapsulation with no class required.
